## What HighLevel does (plain language)

### Takeaway
HighLevel is an all-in-one sales and marketing system for small businesses. It replaces separate tools for CRM, funnels and websites, email and SMS, booking calendars, reviews and AI chat or voice agents. Agencies can also rebrand it as their own software and resell it to their clients. LeadConnector is the unbranded name the platform uses for its mobile app, phone and email services.

### Cited Findings
- HighLevel calls itself "the AI-powered operating system designed to help agencies and SMBs build, operate and scale," covering marketing, sales, communication and operations in "a single, unified system" [official snippet, press release] — [StreetInsider press release "How HighLevel Customers Built Real Momentum in 2025"](https://www.streetinsider.com/Press+Releases/How+HighLevel+Customers+Built+Real+Momentum+in+2025/26087899.html)
- Its core customers are "digital marketing agencies and consultants who purchase HighLevel licenses, white-label the platform under their own branding and custom domains, and resell access and services" to small-business clients [official snippet, press release] — [StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Press+Releases/How+HighLevel+Customers+Built+Real+Momentum+in+2025/26087899.html)
- Scale: "over 2 million businesses" in the community. As of mid-2025 the platform handled more than 15 billion API hits and more than 2.5 billion message events per day [official snippet, press release] — [StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Press+Releases/How+HighLevel+Customers+Built+Real+Momentum+in+2025/26087899.html)
- Structure: one agency account manages many "sub-accounts," one per client business [official snippet, developer docs] — [marketplace.gohighlevel.com: Agency vs Sub-Account](https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/2023-02-21/oauth/AgencyVsSubAccount)
- LeadConnector is a generic brand HighLevel created, with no white-label mention on its public site, so agencies' clients see a neutral name [third-party] — [The Tech Tribe help: What is LeadConnector](https://help.thetechtribe.com/support/solutions/articles/48001226237-what-is-leadconnector)
- "AI Employee" bundles six AI tools: Voice AI, Conversation AI, Reviews AI, Content AI, Funnel AI and Workflow AI Assistant. It is sold as a flat add-on of about $97/month per sub-account with unlimited use, or pay-per-use [third-party] — [ecosire AI Employee guide](https://ecosire.com/fr/blog/gohighlevel-ai-employee-guide)
- Feature set summarized by reviewers: CRM and pipelines, funnels and websites, email and SMS campaigns, calendars, reputation management, workflows, and white-label sub-accounts [third-party] — [martech.zone](https://martech.zone/highlevel-marketing-platform-local-business-agency-white-label)

## Who buys it and why they stay

### Takeaway
The main buyers are marketing agencies and consultants who resell HighLevel to local businesses, followed by coaches, course creators and single local businesses. Customers stay because each client's contacts, automations, funnels, phone numbers and SMS registration all live on the platform. Leaving means rebuilding everything and moving every client.

### Cited Findings
- Core buyers are agencies and consultants who resell to small businesses. Users also include "agencies, operators, consultants and entrepreneurs" [official snippet] — [StreetInsider press release](https://www.streetinsider.com/Press+Releases/How+HighLevel+Customers+Built+Real+Momentum+in+2025/26087899.html)
- Starter suits "a single business or freelancer testing the platform." Unlimited is "the workhorse plan for most agencies." The $497 tier is for reselling HighLevel as your own branded SaaS [third-party] — [Apexure pricing guide](https://www.apexure.com/blog/gohighlevel-pricing)
- Usage-cost rebilling to clients (at cost on Unlimited, with markup and SaaS Mode on Pro) lets agencies earn margin on their clients' messaging [third-party] — [Apexure](https://www.apexure.com/blog/gohighlevel-pricing)

### Inferences
- Switching costs are high. Each client sub-account holds the CRM, workflows, funnels, phone numbers and A2P/10DLC SMS registrations, and SaaS-mode agencies bill their own clients through HighLevel. Leaving means migrating every client. This is my reasoning from the product structure; I found no source with retention or churn figures.

### Gaps
- I found no official churn rate, net revenue retention or customer-segment breakdown.

## Plans and prices (verify on the live pricing page)

### Takeaway
Third-party sources agree on three plans: Starter $97/month, Unlimited $297/month and Agency Pro (formerly SaaS Pro) $497/month. Annual billing is about 17% cheaper (roughly 2 months free). Several 2026 sources say base prices did not rise for 2026. Messaging, phone, email and AI usage is billed separately through a prepaid wallet.

### Cited Findings
- $97 Starter, $297 Unlimited and $497 SaaS Pro/Agency Pro [third-party] — [Apexure](https://www.apexure.com/blog/gohighlevel-pricing); [SupplyGem](https://supplygem.com/gohighlevel-pricing-plans/)
- Annual billing is about 17% off (about 2 months free), which saves nearly $1,000/year on the $497 tier [third-party] — [Apexure](https://www.apexure.com/blog/gohighlevel-pricing)
- Annual pricing is about $80, $247 and $414 per month. The base rates "did not increase for 2026" [third-party] — [rsla.io 2026 pricing](https://rsla.io/blog/go-high-level-pricing); [automatethejourney 2026](https://automatethejourney.com/blog/gohighlevel-pricing-plans-2026)
- **Conflict:** one source says Starter "caps you at three sub-accounts" [third-party, Apexure](https://www.apexure.com/blog/gohighlevel-pricing). Older descriptions say Starter is a single location [third-party summary, same search]. Not verified.
- Unlimited adds unlimited sub-accounts, API access and a branded desktop app. Pro adds the SaaS configurator, rebilling with a markup multiplier and client self-signup by credit card [third-party] — [Apexure](https://www.apexure.com/blog/gohighlevel-pricing)

## Free trial and how a referral signs up

### Takeaway
The standard trial is 14 days. Many affiliates advertise a 30-day trial link. The referral flow is: click the affiliate link, land on a trial page, pick a plan, start the trial, then pay once the trial ends.

### Cited Findings
- Standard trial is 14 days if you go straight to gohighlevel.com. Some affiliate links give an extended 30-day trial, and the landing page states the length before payment details are entered [third-party] — [automatethejourney free trial 2026](https://automatethejourney.com/blog/gohighlevel-free-trial-2026); [growthable](https://www.growthable.io/30-day-trial-of-gohighlevel/); [ghlexperts](https://ghlexperts.com/gohighlevel-free-trial)

## Commission math (40% Tier 1, 5% Tier 2)

### Takeaway
Tier 1 pays 40% recurring for the life of the customer. Tier 2 pays an extra 5% recurring on paying agencies signed up by agencies you referred. The $297 to $118.80/month example appears widely, but I could not confirm it on a HighLevel page.

### Cited Findings
- Tier 1: "40% monthly recurring commission for every paying agency customer you send." Tier 2: "an additional 5% monthly recurring commission for every paying agency signed up by an agency that you signed up" [third-party, closely paraphrasing official wording] — [UpPromote directory](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/highlevel/); [HighLevel Affiliate Terms (old)](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-terms-old)
- Example: an agency customer on Unlimited at $297/month earns you $118.80 every month they stay [third-party] — [passivesecrets](https://passivesecrets.com/gohighlevel-affiliate-program/); [thestackinsiders](https://www.thestackinsiders.com/blog/gohighlevel-affiliate-program)
- Payouts are monthly, around the 15th, for the previous month's commissions. Minimum payout is $50 [third-party] — [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/highlevel/)
- HighLevel decides qualification at its "sole discretion" and may pause or withhold payments over transaction concerns [official snippet] — [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)

**Calculated commissions (my arithmetic, not sourced):**

| Plan | Monthly price | 40% Tier 1 | 5% Tier 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $97 | $38.80/month | $4.85/month |
| Unlimited | $297 | $118.80/month | $14.85/month |
| Agency Pro | $497 | $198.80/month | $24.85/month |

On annual plans, commission would be about 17% lower per month.

### Inferences
- **Ambiguity on Tier 2:** "5% for every paying agency signed up by an agency that you signed up" most likely means 5% of the Tier-2 customer's subscription. One snippet instead says "5% … on commissions earned by the affiliates you refer," which would mean 5% of 40%, or 2% of revenue. Confirm in the current agreement.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether HighLevel's own affiliate page uses the $118.80 example.
- I could not confirm whether Tier 2 requires the referred agency to also be an affiliate.

## Common complaints and churn reasons

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints are:
- a steep learning curve
- inconsistent support
- surprise usage-based charges (SMS and AI)
- email deliverability

Headline ratings are high but could not be verified directly.

### Cited Findings
- The learning curve is "the most consistently cited friction point." Reviewers estimate 6 to 8 weeks before a user feels confident [third-party aggregator, 2026] — [autogencrm 2026](https://autogencrm.com/?p=3950); [ghlscaleup review](https://www.ghlscaleup.com/blog/gohighlevel-review)
- Support complaints include long waits, uninformed agents and difficulty reaching support. Support is rated about 3.5/5 [third-party] — [oneexpand review](https://oneexpand.com/gohighlevel-review/); [creatoreconomytools](https://creatoreconomytools.com/tool/gohighlevel)
- Billing complaints include unexpected charges from usage-based AI and SMS [third-party] — [velpoint reviews](https://velpoint.it.com/reviews/)
- Trustpilot is reported as 4.9/5 with more than 13,000 reviews. Another source gives 4.1 to 4.6 across G2, Capterra and Trustpilot [third-party, unverified] — [velpoint](https://velpoint.it.com/reviews/); [G2 reviews page (not opened)](https://g2.com/products/highlevel/reviews?page=11)

### Inferences
- A very high Trustpilot score alongside heavy support complaints may reflect HighLevel prompting customers for reviews. That is speculation.
