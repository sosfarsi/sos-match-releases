## 1. Official URLs and which terms apply

### Takeaway
HighLevel's affiliate terms exist in at least three generations:
- **`/affiliate-terms-old`**: titled "HighLevel Affiliate Terms of Service". This is the legacy version, with "Sale/Legacy Sale/Legacy Bonus" language.
- **`/affiliate-terms`**: titled "HighLevel Affiliate Agreement".
- **`/affiliate-policy`**: titled "HighLevel Affiliate Program Agreement" in some results and "HighLevel Affiliate Program Services Agreement" in more recent ones. It works together with a separate **"Program Policies"** page at `/affiliate-policy/program-rules`.

The `/affiliate-policy` agreement plus program-rules pair appears to be the current one that new affiliates accept. Third parties date it "June 2026", and they date the Program Policies as effective 7 Nov 2025. I could not confirm either date on the official pages.

## 2. Eligibility, onboarding, tax and payouts

### Takeaway
Signup is self-serve, and third parties say access is immediate. The legacy terms reserve HighLevel's sole discretion over who qualifies.

Payouts:
- go out on the 15th of each month, for commissions earned in the previous month
- go out through Tipalti, once accrued commissions exceed $50

A commission counts only after the referred account stays in good standing for 45 days.

### Cited Findings
- Payout timing and minimum: "HighLevel approves and sends all payments on the 15th of each month for all commissions earned the previous month." If the 15th falls on a weekend or holiday, payment goes out the next business day. "There is a $50 minimum for a payout... As soon as your total commissions accrued is higher than $50 you will receive a payout." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Payouts help article](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001208136-affiliate-payouts-where-how-when-can-i-get-paid-)
- Payout platform: "As of now all Payouts are through Tipalti. You must set up Tipalti in order to choose your payment method and start receiving Affiliate payouts." [official snippet] — same help article
- Payout methods via Tipalti are direct deposit, PayPal or debit card. [third-party] — [botpenguin/ghlfocus summaries](https://botpenguin.com/blogs/gohighlevel-affiliate-program)
- From the agreement: "HighLevel may hold payments until your earned Commissions exceed $50.00." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)
- Forfeiture under the older terms: "If combined commissions in a 120-day period do not exceed $50.00 (USD), commissions will not be paid and will be forfeited." [official snippet; probably /affiliate-terms or /affiliate-terms-old, attribution unclear] — [Affiliate Agreement](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-terms)
- Commissions "may be forfeited if HighLevel is unable to submit payment to your payment account for any reason." [attribution unclear, likely official agreement] — [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)
- Holding and discretion: HighLevel "decides whether a purchase qualifies for a commission in its sole discretion," and "if HighLevel has questions or concerns about a transaction, it can pause or withhold commission payments." [official snippet, paraphrased by the search tool] — [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)
- Approval: "There is no approval process or waiting period, and access is granted immediately upon signup" at affiliate.gohighlevel.com. [third-party] — [consultevo](https://consultevo.com/gohighlevel-affiliate-portal-guide/). The legacy terms, by contrast, say you must "provide all information requested by HighLevel in connection with your Affiliate application" and that HighLevel decides eligibility at its "sole and exclusive discretion". [official snippet] — [Affiliate Terms (old)](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-terms-old)
- Tax forms: a snippet about W-9 / W-8BEN and a $600+ threshold matched, but it describes the **Affiliate Manager** product (agencies collecting tax forms from *their* affiliates). It is **not** a HighLevel-program rule. [official snippet, different product] — [How to Manage Affiliate Payouts and Commissions](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000003655-how-to-manage-affiliate-payouts-and-commissions)

### Inferences
- Tipalti onboarding normally collects W-9 or W-8 forms. That is likely how HighLevel gathers tax information, but I found no direct source for it.

### Gaps
- Whether an affiliate must be a paying HighLevel customer: no source found. Signup appears open to anyone.
- Country restrictions or sanctioned-country exclusions: no source found.
- Payout currency (presumably USD, since the agreement states $50.00 USD): not confirmed for the current agreement.
- Whether the 120-day forfeiture rule survives in the current `/affiliate-policy` agreement is unconfirmed.

## 3. Commission rules

### Takeaway
The standard rate is **40% recurring** on direct referrals to "eligible" products, paid for as long as the account stays active. **Tier 2 is 5%**, and the current agreement reportedly applies it only when HighLevel approves a second-tier designation.

A "Qualified Purchase" requires that the customer:
- clicked your Affiliate Link
- completed a new purchase
- stayed in good standing for 45 days (an account paused during that window does not qualify)

Attribution is reportedly last-click with a 90-day window. Self-referrals are banned. Refunds and chargebacks are deducted from future payouts.

### Cited Findings
- Definition: "Qualified Purchase means a completed purchase of HighLevel products by a new or existing customer who: (i) clicked your Affiliate Link; (ii) completed the purchase; and (iii) met all other eligibility requirements in this Agreement and the Program Policies." "Commission means the payment you may earn for a Qualified Purchase." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)
- Second formulation: "A purchase is a 'Qualified Purchase' only if a customer (1) clicks your unique Affiliate Link; (2) completes a new purchase; and (3) maintains their account in good standing for at least 45 days." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)
- Paused accounts: "a paused account during that period does not qualify." [third-party, citing the June 2026 agreement] — [ghlfocus](https://ghlfocus.com/gohighlevel-affiliate-program/); [ghlbuilds](https://ghlbuilds.com/highlevel-affiliate-program-agreement/)
- Rates:
  - "40% monthly recurring commission from everyone you introduce to HighLevel." [official snippet] — [First Steps help article](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001182420-first-steps-to-be-a-successful-highlevel-affiliate)
  - "40% lifetime commissions on paid HighLevel accounts you directly refer, with commissions accruing as long as referred accounts remain active." [official snippet] — help.gohighlevel.com (same search)
- Eligible categories: the June 2026 agreement lists "a standard 40% direct-referral commission on specified eligible categories," and "The standard second-tier rate is 5% when HighLevel approves the second-tier designation." [third-party, citing the agreement] — [ghlfocus](https://ghlfocus.com/gohighlevel-affiliate-program/)
- Applies to the Starter ($97), Agency Freelancer/Unlimited ($297) and Agency Pro ($497) plans. [third-party] — [ghlfocus](https://ghlfocus.com/gohighlevel-affiliate-program/)
- Attribution:
  - "90-day standard last-click attribution window... a later eligible affiliate click can affect which affiliate receives attribution." [third-party, said to cite the agreement] — [ghlfocus / netpartners](https://netpartners.marketing/highlevel-affiliate-payouts-explained/)
  - Another third party describes the window as "90 days from the first click". [third-party] — [oneexpand](https://oneexpand.com/gohighlevel-affiliate-program/)
  - Some older reviews say 30–60 days. [third-party] — [affililist](https://www.affililist.com/affiliate/gohighlevel)
  - Treat 90 days, last-click, as current, but it is unconfirmed against official text.
- Tracking parameter: links append `?fp_ref=[your-id]`. [third-party] — [oneexpand](https://oneexpand.com/gohighlevel-affiliate-program/)
- Self-referral: affiliates "cannot use their own affiliate link to purchase a HighLevel subscription for themselves," which is "considered fraudulent activity that may result in termination and forfeiture of commissions." [official snippet] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)
- Refunds and chargebacks:
  - Current version: "If a commission was already paid and the sale is refunded or charged back, it's deducted from your future payouts." [attribution unclear; probably a netpartners summary of the agreement] — [netpartners payouts](https://netpartners.marketing/highlevel-affiliate-payouts-explained/)
  - Legacy wording: "If HighLevel determines, in its sole and exclusive discretion, that any Sale or Legacy Sale was procured fraudulently or as a result of any violation of this Agreement or applicable law, no Commission or Legacy Bonus will be paid... for past sales, such payment amounts shall be deducted from Your future Commissions and Legacy Bonuses." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Terms (old)](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-terms-old)
- Tier 2 means you earn 5% recurring on sales made by affiliates you recruit. [third-party] — [botpenguin](https://botpenguin.com/blogs/gohighlevel-affiliate-program)
- Affiliate Commander status goes to affiliates generating "at least six figures in annual commission from new referrals and/or upgrades." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Commander](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001728-the-affiliate-commander-program)

### Inferences
- "New purchase" combined with "new or existing customer" suggests that upgrades by existing customers can qualify, consistent with the Commander article's mention of "upgrades". Whether an existing *trial* or lead counts depends on the click and attribution rules. Unverified.

### Gaps
- Full list of eligible and excluded categories. I found no official text on SaaS-mode rebilling, wallet or usage charges (LC Phone/Email, AI), marketplace items, or add-ons.
- Coupon usage rules for buyers.
- Exact cookie length and first-click vs last-click in official wording.
- Whether the 45-day period is also the payout holding period.
- Clawback window length.

## 4. Prohibited activities

### Takeaway
The Program Policies prohibit all of the following:
- brand bidding on HighLevel, GoHighLevel, GHL and SaaSPRENEUR, including coupon, discount, promo and deal variations, combined terms, and use in ad title, copy, display name or URL, unless HighLevel consents in writing
- trademark use in domains or social handles, modified logos, and implying you work for HighLevel
- false, misleading or exaggerated claims, including about results
- spam: unsolicited mass email, SMS or DMs, with CAN-SPAM and TCPA compliance required
- cookie stuffing, pop-ups and other fraud
- self-referral
- sub-affiliate networks or letting third parties use your link, unless agreed in writing
- disparaging HighLevel
- calling HighLevel a "business opportunity", franchise, or "business-in-a-box"

### Cited Findings
- Brand bidding:
  - Affiliates "cannot bid on terms such as HighLevel, GoHighLevel, GHL, SaaSPRENEUR, or branded coupon, discount, promo, and deal-related variations unless HighLevel gives written consent."
  - They "cannot combine restricted terms with other search terms or use them in ad title, copy, display name, or display URL."
  - HighLevel recommends adding its trademarked terms as negative keywords.
  - [official snippet] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)
- Trademarks: affiliates "may only use HighLevel's trademarks and brand assets as permitted, may not modify logos, use the name in a way that implies employment with HighLevel, or use the brand in domain names or social media handles." [official snippet] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)
- Honesty: "never make false, misleading, or exaggerated claims about HighLevel, its products, or what users can achieve"; promotions must be "truthful and based on actual experience." [official snippet] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)
- Fraud: affiliates "must not use cookie-stuffing, pop-ups, or other fraudulent techniques to generate commissions." [official snippet] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)
- Spam: "prohibits... any form of spamming, including unsolicited mass emails, texts, or social media messages," and all communications must comply with CAN-SPAM and the TCPA. [official snippet] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)
- Laws: affiliates must comply with "the FTC Endorsement Guides, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, and state telemarketing laws." [official snippet] — [Program Policies / Agreement](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)
- Sub-affiliates: "Unless we agree otherwise in writing, do not promote HighLevel through a sub-affiliate network or let any third party use your affiliate link." [attribution unclear, likely Program Policies] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)
- Claims scope: affiliates are "strictly prohibited from making claims concerning products and services that are inconsistent with, or beyond the scope of marketing materials produced by HighLevel," and "expressly prohibited from claiming that HighLevel is or provides a business opportunity, franchise opportunity, a 'business-in-a-box,' or an assisted marketing plan." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)
- Disparagement: affiliates agree "not to make defamatory or disparaging remarks, comments or statements concerning HighLevel or any employees or officers." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)
- Conduct: "Conduct that HighLevel determines reflects poorly on the Program or the HighLevel community may result in corrective action... up to and including removal from the Program and forfeiture of unpaid Commissions." [official snippet] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)

### Inferences
- Coupon and deal sites are effectively restricted: you cannot bid on coupon or promo variants of the brand. I found no explicit ban on listing on coupon sites themselves.

### Gaps
- Direct linking (ads pointing straight to gohighlevel.com with an affiliate link): no explicit text found.
- Incentivised signups or rebates and cashback: no text found.
- Rules on content types (adult, hate content and similar) and on impersonating HighLevel support: no text found.
- Complete banned-keyword list beyond the four terms above: unconfirmed.

## 6. Termination, consequences, changes and dispute resolution

### Takeaway
Participation runs month to month. Either party can terminate with 30 days' written notice, with or without cause. That provision is in the legacy terms and is likely carried forward.

If you violate the Policies, HighLevel may:
- suspend Commission payments
- revoke benefits
- restrict event participation
- remove you and forfeit unpaid Commissions

Disputes go to binding arbitration in Dallas, Texas, with a class-action waiver.

### Cited Findings
- Notice: "Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by giving the other Party thirty (30) days' written notice of termination." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Terms (old)](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-terms-old)
- Term: participation "continues month-to-month until terminated." [attribution unclear] — same search
- Policy violations: "A violation of the Policies is a violation of your Affiliate Program Services Agreement, and if you break these rules, HighLevel may suspend Commission payments, revoke Program benefits, restrict event participation, or remove you from the Program and forfeit unpaid Commissions." [official snippet] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)
- Brand keywords: violating the paid-ads and brand-keyword rules "can result in commission forfeiture." [attribution unclear] — [Program Policies](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy/program-rules)
- Disputes: "binding arbitration in Dallas, Texas and includes a class action waiver." [official snippet] — [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)
- Inactivity: the search returned "HighLevel reserves the right to delete Platform Accounts that have remained inactive for ninety (90) days or more." The "Platform Accounts" wording suggests this comes from the **general Terms of Service**, not the affiliate agreement. Do not cite it as an affiliate inactivity rule. [official snippet, probably the wrong document] — [Terms of Service](https://www.gohighlevel.com/terms-of-service)

### Gaps
- Affiliate-specific inactivity or dormancy rule: none found.
- HighLevel's right to amend the terms, and how notice is given: not found in snippets, though such clauses are typical.
- Whether termination for convenience forfeits commissions that were earned but not yet paid: unconfirmed.

## 7. Recent changes (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
The structure moved from the legacy "Affiliate Terms of Service" (Sales, Legacy Bonus) to the `/affiliate-policy` "Program Agreement / Services Agreement" plus a standalone Program Policies page. Third parties date the policies effective 7 Nov 2025 and the agreement to June 2026.

The changes that appear new are:
- the 45-day good-standing requirement
- the paused-account exclusion
- "eligible categories"
- Tier 2 only with HighLevel approval
- the explicit brand-bidding list, including SaaSPRENEUR

The headline 40% and 5% rates appear unchanged.

### Cited Findings
- Policies effective 7 Nov 2025. [third-party / attribution unclear] — [ghlfocus](https://ghlfocus.com/gohighlevel-affiliate-program/)
- Agreement updated June 2026. [third-party] — [ghlbuilds](https://ghlbuilds.com/highlevel-affiliate-program-agreement/)
- Main Terms of Service and Privacy updates effective 22 Sep 2025. [attribution unclear] — [Platform Updates help article](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006344-highlevel-platform-updates-what-s-changed)
