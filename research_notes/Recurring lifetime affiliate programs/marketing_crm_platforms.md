# Recurring Affiliate Programs: Marketing Automation, CRM, Chat-Marketing and Agency Platforms (as of Oct 2026)

**How this was checked (read first):** The network proxy in this research environment blocked direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl) to every official domain tried: gohighlevel.com, manychat.com, help.manychat.com, partnerstack.com, getresponse.com, kit.com, hubspot.com, pipedrive.com, instantly.ai and others. So "verification" here means search-engine-indexed text that the search tool attributed to the official URL (search restricted to the vendor's own domain where possible). Those claims are marked **[official-domain snippet]**. Claims that came only from third-party directories or blogs are marked **[third-party, unconfirmed]**. Before running ads, the user should open each official terms page and re-check it. Terms can change without notice, and many programs say so.

## Q1. Which programs pay 25%+ recurring for the lifetime of the customer today?

### Takeaway
Only a few programs in this set clearly pay 25%+ for the customer's lifetime from day one: **HighLevel (40% lifetime, plus 5% tier-2)**, **AWeber (30% to 50%, paid every time the referral pays)** and **Moosend (30% to 40% for as long as the referral pays)**. **Instantly** markets "40% lifetime" but reportedly starts at 20%. **Smartlead** is lifetime but starts at 15%. **Close** pays lifetime 30% only to higher-tier partners, capped at $10k per customer. Most others (ManyChat, Chatfuel, ActiveCampaign, Kit, GetResponse, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Keap, lemlist, SocialPilot, Vendasta) cap the term at 12 or 24 months, or pay a flat bounty.

## Q2. Which programs changed terms in 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
Confirmed changes: **Close** raised its rate from 20% to 30% for referrals from Feb 1, 2025. **GetResponse** moved to "40% for 12 months" (described as up from 33% recurring) and moved to PartnerStack. **ManyChat** changed its plan pricing to an "Active Contacts" model, which directly changes what affiliates earn. **HubSpot** revamped its program (180-day cookie, tiers; date not confirmed). Several third-party listings show different figures from the official ones, which suggests recent changes.

### Cited Findings
- Close: 20% for referrals before Feb 1, 2025, and 30% for 12 months from Feb 1, 2025, plus lifetime 30% for higher tiers (capped at $10k) — [Close Partner Program Agreement](https://www.close.com/partners/partner-program-agreement)
- GetResponse: the "glow-up" announcement says the base commission is now 40% for 12 months (up from 33% recurring) and the program moved to PartnerStack. The old 33% was widely described as lifetime, which is a change from lifetime to a 12-month cap — [GetResponse blog](https://www.getresponse.com/blog/getresponse-affiliate-program)
- ManyChat Pro is now priced per Active Contact: $39/mo monthly ($29 annual) for up to 2,500 active contacts, with overage at $0.05 per contact ($0.038 annual) — [ManyChat Help: Pro plan](https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/25800228332572-Pro-plan)
- ManyChat cookie: the official terms say 90 days from first click, while third-party directories say 120 days, so the old figure appears to have been replaced — [ManyChat Affiliate Terms](https://manychat.com/legal/affiliate-program-terms); [getreditus (120 days)](https://getreditus.com/affiliate-programs/manychat)
- HubSpot "doubled the cookie window to 180 days" as part of the revamp; the announcement date was not captured — [HubSpot company news](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/hubspot-launches-its-revamped-affiliate-program)
- AWeber announced Advocates can now earn up to 50% (date not captured) — [AWeber blog](https://blog.aweber.com/updates/advocates-earn-up-to-50-commissions.htm)
- Kit added post-year-one 10–20% recurring for status affiliates on top of 50% for 12 months (date not captured) — [Kit Affiliate](https://kit.com/affiliate)
- HighLevel has an "affiliate-terms-old" page alongside a current "affiliate-policy" (Affiliate Program Services Agreement), so the agreement was revised (date unknown) — [HighLevel old terms](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-terms-old); [HighLevel current agreement](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-policy)

### Inferences
- The trend is toward shorter durations (GetResponse, ManyChat and HubSpot all cap at 12 months) and volume tiers. HighLevel, AWeber and Moosend have kept lifetime terms.

### Gaps
- Exact effective dates for the HubSpot, AWeber, Kit, GetResponse and HighLevel changes were not confirmed.
- No evidence was found either way on whether HighLevel's 40% has changed in 2026; official snippets still say 40% plus 5%.

## Q3. Which programs ban or restrict PPC / brand-keyword bidding? (critical for a Google Ads user)

### Takeaway
Every program with retrievable terms bans **brand-keyword bidding**. **GetResponse** goes further and bans paid search and social ads entirely without written consent, and bans direct linking even on non-brand keywords. Assume brand bidding is banned everywhere. Non-brand PPC to your own landing page is allowed or unstated at most programs. Any program not listed below must be checked before spending.

### Cited Findings
- **HighLevel**: no bidding on "Restricted Terms" on Google, Bing, Yahoo, Facebook or any network without written consent. The terms include HighLevel, GoHighLevel, GHL, gohighlevel.com, "HighLevel coupon / discount / promo code / deals", SaaSPRENEUR, any misspellings or variations, and any future terms HighLevel adds — [HighLevel Affiliate Agreement](https://www.gohighlevel.com/affiliate-terms); summarized at [netpartners.marketing](https://netpartners.marketing/gohighlevel-affiliate-brand-bidding-ppc-policy/)
- **ManyChat**: paid search ads that use ManyChat trademarks or variations as bidding terms are banned without prior written authorization. Cookie stuffing, hidden iframes and forced redirects count as fraud — [ManyChat Affiliate Program Terms](https://manychat.com/legal/affiliate-program-terms)
- **ActiveCampaign**: you may not buy or bid on any keywords that include ActiveCampaign marks, branding, variations or misspellings — [ActiveCampaign Affiliate Partner Terms](https://www.activecampaign.com/legal/affiliate-partner-terms)
- **GetResponse**: no promotion through paid ads (Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Google AdWords) without prior written consent. No bidding on GetResponse trademarks or combinations of them. Non-branded keywords may not link directly to GetResponse; ads must go to the affiliate's own landing page — [GetResponse Affiliate Terms of Service](https://www.getresponse.com/legal/affiliate-terms-of-service)
- **Kit** has an official help article on what affiliate marketing is and isn't allowed; its contents were not retrieved — [Kit Help](https://help.kit.com/en/articles/4881296-what-type-of-affiliate-marketing-is-and-isn-t-allowed-at-kit)

### Inferences
- For a Google Ads user, the workable model is non-brand, problem or solution keywords (for example "CRM for agencies" or "Instagram DM automation") sent to the user's own review or comparison page, not straight to the vendor. Because of GetResponse's blanket paid-ads ban, it should be excluded from PPC unless it gives written approval.
- PartnerStack-hosted programs (ManyChat, Close, Instantly, GetResponse, lemlist, Keap) usually include similar trademark clauses, but that is not verified per program.

### Gaps
- PPC and brand terms were not retrieved for AWeber, Moosend, Brevo, Klaviyo, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Close, Keap, Instantly, lemlist, Smartlead, Vendasta, SocialPilot, Agorapulse, Chatfuel or Kit (contents). Each needs a manual read of its terms.

## Q4. ManyChat: what does it pay, and does the "$600k/month" story hold up?

### Takeaway
ManyChat pays **30% / 40% / 50% for 12 months only**, tiered by lifetime paid signups (0–30, 31–200, 201+). It has a 90-day attribution window, pays through PartnerStack on the 13th of the month about two months after the referral pays, and **bans brand bidding**. **No source was found for an affiliate earning "$600k/month" from ManyChat, so the claim is unverifiable.** The math also makes it very unlikely.

### Cited Findings
- Tiers: Gold 30% (0–30 paid signups), Sapphire 40% (31–200), Diamond 50% (201+), all for 12 months. The program is uncapped — [ManyChat Partner FAQ](https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/20518248843164-Manychat-Partner-Program-FAQ); [ManyChat Affiliate Terms](https://manychat.com/legal/affiliate-program-terms); [ManyChat Affiliate page](https://manychat.com/affiliate/)
- Payout: via PartnerStack. A commission is approved one month after the referral pays and paid on the 13th; for example, a payment on Apr 15 is approved May 15 and paid Jun 13 — [ManyChat Partner FAQ](https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/20518248843164-Manychat-Partner-Program-FAQ)
- Third-party sources add Stripe or PayPal payout via PartnerStack [third-party] — [getreditus](https://getreditus.com/affiliate-programs/manychat)
- Attribution window: 90 days from first click (official terms). Third-party sites say 120 days, so they conflict — [ManyChat Affiliate Terms](https://manychat.com/legal/affiliate-program-terms)
- Pro plan: $39/mo ($29 annual) for 2,500 active contacts, then $0.05 per extra contact — [ManyChat Help: Pro plan](https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/25800228332572-Pro-plan)
- A search for "ManyChat affiliate $600k month" found no case study, payout proof or official statement supporting the figure — [search results incl. getreditus, botpenguin, getlasso](https://getreditus.com/affiliate-programs/manychat)

### Inferences
- **Plausibility math (illustrative):** At the Diamond 50% rate on a $39 Pro plan, each customer pays the affiliate $19.50/mo. $600,000/mo would need about 30,800 concurrently paying referrals, all acquired within the last 12 months because commissions stop after a year, and all at the top tier. At a $100/mo average account it would still need about 12,000 active referrals inside 12 months. That is very unlikely for one affiliate.
- The story most likely mixes up something else: total ManyChat affiliate payouts across all partners, revenue from the person's own business that uses ManyChat (for example Instagram DM funnels selling their own products), or HighLevel-style lifetime earnings. **Flag as unverified.**
- For an affiliate, ManyChat's best features are its brand and conversion rate. The 12-month cap and low entry price mean each customer is worth about $140 (Gold) to $234 (Diamond) on the $39 plan.

### Gaps
- The original source of the "$600k/month" story was not identified.
- ManyChat's official affiliate page and PartnerStack listing could not be fetched directly. The figures come from official-domain search snippets, which agreed with each other.
