# Recurring Affiliate Programs Outside Course/CRM Categories (SEO, hosting, builders, ecommerce, video/webinar, AI, PM, finance, field service). Researched 2026-10-07

**Method note:** The research proxy blocked direct fetches of every official affiliate page tried (seranking.com, webflow.com, surferseo.com, demio.com, streamyard.com, recurringcommission.com). Where possible, facts come from web searches limited to the vendor's own domain, so the quoted text comes from official pages and is marked **[official-domain snippet]**. Everything else comes from aggregators or blogs and is marked **[third-party, unconfirmed]**. The user should open each official page before choosing a program, since the terms of programs like these change often.

**How the estimates work:** "Comm./customer/mo" = commission % × the monthly plan price. "Customers to reach $100–150/mo" = the number of paying referrals needed for that much monthly recurring commission. Programs that pay for a limited time stop paying after the stated period.

## Which programs meet the bar: 25%+ recurring, ideally lifetime, product $100+/mo?

### Takeaway
Only **SE Ranking** clearly meets every criterion: 30% for the customer's lifetime, a 120-day cookie, and a main plan around $100–130+/mo. **StreamYard** pays 25% for as long as the customer stays subscribed, but only its Business plan costs over $100/mo. **Demio** pays 25% for up to 3 years, but on plans under $100/mo. **Pabbly** pays 30% lifetime with a 365-day cookie, but its prices are low. Several strong programs pay 30–50% for only 12 months (Webflow, Make, Notion, Riverside, Jasper, WebinarJam), and the hosting programs pay lifetime commissions below 25% (Kinsta 10%, Cloudways 7%).

### Cited Findings
**Programs that qualify or nearly qualify**
- **SE Ranking**: 30% commission on each subscription sale, and "all of the user's subsequent payments and rebills" also earn 30%. This is a lifetime recurring commission. **[official-domain snippet]** — [SE Ranking affiliate page](https://seranking.com/affiliate.html); [SE Ranking affiliate terms](https://seranking.com/legal/affiliate.html)
  - Cookie: 120 days. Once the user signs up, the affiliate is "forever credited" as the referrer. — [SE Ranking help: Affiliate program FAQ](https://help.seranking.com/hc/en-us/articles/16332910001180-Affiliate-program-FAQ)
  - Payout: every 14 days when the balance is over $50. **[official-domain snippet]** — [SE Ranking affiliate terms](https://seranking.com/legal/affiliate.html)
  - Pricing **(conflicting, unconfirmed)**: one source says 2026 plans are Core $129/mo ($103.20/mo billed annually) and Growth $279/mo ($223.20/mo annually). Older sources list Essential $52, Pro $95.20 and Business $207.20 per month billed annually. — [costbench](https://costbench.com/software/ai-seo-tools/se-ranking/); [seo.bargains](https://seo.bargains/se-ranking-pricing-breakdown/)
  - Estimate: Core $129 × 30% = **$38.70/customer/mo**, so **3–4 customers** for $100–150/mo. Growth $279 × 30% = $83.70, so **2 customers**.
- **StreamYard**: 25% commission on subscriptions, recurring "as long as the person remains subscribed." **[official-domain snippet]** — [StreamYard Affiliate Terms](https://support.streamyard.com/hc/en-us/articles/31889400259092-Affiliate-Terms); [StreamYard partners](https://partners.streamyard.com/)
  - Pricing: Advanced $88.99/mo ($68.99/mo billed annually); Business $299/mo for 2–10 seats. **[third-party summary of the pricing page]** — [StreamYard pricing](https://streamyard.com/pricing)
  - Estimate: Advanced plan = **$22.25/mo** (5–7 customers needed). Business plan = **$74.75/mo** (2 customers needed).
- **Demio**: 25% of each monthly payment, for **up to 3 years**, with a 60-day cookie. **[official-domain snippet]** — [Demio: Become an Affiliate](https://www.demio.com/become-an-affiliate)
  - Pricing: Starter $49/mo, Growth $99/mo ($69/mo billed annually). **[third-party]** — [OMR Demio pricing](https://omr.com/en/reviews/product/demio/pricing)
  - Estimate: Growth plan = **$24.75/mo**, so 4–6 customers needed. Payments end after 36 months.
- **Pabbly**: 30% recurring "forever" for as long as the customer stays active, with a 365-day cookie. **[official-domain snippet]** — [Pabbly Affiliate Program](https://www.pabbly.com/affiliates/); [Pabbly forum Affiliate FAQs](https://forum.pabbly.com/threads/affiliate-faqs-https-www-pabbly-com-affiliates.12193/)
  - Pabbly's plan prices were not verified (see Gaps). The terms are lifetime, but Pabbly is generally priced below $100/mo, so it likely misses the price criterion.

**Programs that pay 25%+ for a limited time (usually 12 months)**
- **Webflow**: 50% of a new customer's first subscription for **up to 12 months** on a monthly plan, or on the first annual plan purchase. Pro affiliates earn +10%, and Premium affiliates +15%, for **up to 12 more months** if the customer renews. 90-day cookie with first-touch attribution. **[official-domain snippet]** — [Webflow Affiliate Program](https://webflow.com/solutions/affiliates); [Webflow help: program overview](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961372613011-Webflow-s-affiliate-program-overview); [Webflow Affiliate Agreement](https://webflow.com/legal/affiliate-terms)
- **Make**: 35% of the customer's payments "for the next 12 months." **[official-domain snippet]** — [Make affiliate](https://www.make.com/en/affiliate); [Make Help Center](https://help.make.com/affiliate-program)
- **WebinarJam**: "up to 40% recurring commission for up to 12 months." **[official-domain snippet]** — [WebinarJam partners](https://webinarjam.com/partners/)
- **Notion**: 50% of payments for the first 12 months on Plus, Business or AI plans, with a 180-day cookie. **[third-party, unconfirmed]** — [supademo roundup](https://supademo.com/blog/saas-affiliate-programs)
- **Riverside**: 30% recurring for 12 months, 60-day cookie. **[third-party, unconfirmed]** — [supademo](https://supademo.com/blog/saas-affiliate-programs)
- **Jasper**: 25% recurring for 12 months, rising to 30% after 100 customers. 45-day cookie. **[third-party, unconfirmed]** — [automatetoprofit](https://automatetoprofit.com/jasper-affiliate-program-commission/)

**Programs with recurring commissions under 25%**
- **Kinsta**: a one-time payment of $50–$500, plus **10% monthly for the customer's lifetime**. The 10% is calculated on the plan fee after discounts and excludes add-ons. 60-day cookie. Paid by PayPal monthly (between the 15th and 20th) once the balance reaches $50. **[official-domain search result; third-party summary]** — [Kinsta Docs: Affiliate Program](https://kinsta.com/docs/service-information/affiliate-program/); [Kinsta Affiliates FAQ](https://kinsta.com/affiliates/faq/)
- **Cloudways**: the Hybrid plan pays **$30 upfront plus 7% of the referral's monthly invoice for life**. A separate Slab plan pays flat commissions of up to $125 per sale. **[official-domain snippet]** — [Cloudways affiliate](https://www.cloudways.com/en/web-hosting-affiliate-program.php); [Cloudways support FAQ](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/5134056-how-to-join-cloudways-affiliate-program-faqs-benefits)
- **Writesonic**: 20% recurring for up to 12 months, 60-day cookie. **[official-domain snippet]** — [Writesonic affiliate](https://writesonic.com/affiliate)
- **Descript**: 15% recurring for the first year. The program has reportedly moved to a flat rate for new referrals. **[official-domain search result; the move to a flat rate is unconfirmed]** — [Descript affiliate terms](https://www.descript.com/affiliate-terms)
- **monday.com**: 20% recurring, 90-day cookie. **[third-party, unconfirmed]** — [supademo](https://supademo.com/blog/saas-affiliate-programs)
- **Gusto**: channel partners earn up to 20% revenue share. This is a partner/accountant program, not an open affiliate program. **[official-domain snippet]** — [Gusto Channel Partner Program](https://gusto.com/partners/channel)
- **Jobber**: sources conflict. One says 20% recurring with a 90-day cookie. Another lists flat amounts per plan: $5 per signup, $50–$300 per annual sale, or 60% of a monthly sale. **[third-party, conflicting]** — [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/jobber/); [referralrocket](https://referralrocket.io/affiliate-program/jobber_VP)

### Inferences
- For a lifetime commission on a product costing over $100/mo, SE Ranking is the strongest choice. Two to four retained customers would bring in $100–150/mo, if the 2026 Core/Growth prices are accurate.
- Webflow's 50% for 12 months on a high-tier plan can pay more in the first year than a 25% lifetime program. However, it pays little or nothing after year 1 (or year 2 at the Pro/Premium tiers), so it does not build lasting income.
- Hosting programs (Kinsta, Cloudways) pay lifetime commissions, but at 7–10%. Hosting customers tend to stay a long time, though, so a large account (for example, a Kinsta client paying several hundred dollars a month) could still earn a meaningful amount each month.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm SE Ranking's current plan names and prices on the official page (fetch was blocked; sources conflict).
- Pabbly plan prices, payout threshold and PPC rules were not retrieved.
- Notion, Riverside, Jasper, monday.com, Livestorm and Calendly terms were not confirmed on official pages. No data at all was found for Livestorm or Calendly.
- Surfer: the official page is titled "Earn up to 125% Commission." A third-party summary describes tiers paying 75–125% one-time on monthly plans and 15–25% of yearly plans. Whether any part of this recurs could not be confirmed — [Surfer affiliate page](https://surferseo.com/affiliate-program/).
- No affiliate terms were found for Vagaro, Mindbody, Acuity or Bench.

## Which programs pay flat bounties instead (for contrast)?

### Takeaway
Most of the large platforms pay **one-time bounties**, not recurring commissions: Semrush, Shopify, BigCommerce, Wix, WP Engine, FreshBooks, QuickBooks, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Hostinger, Elementor, ClickUp and Canva. Ahrefs and Zapier have no public affiliate program.

### Cited Findings
- **Semrush**: pays per sale, not recurring. $300 per Semrush One sale; $200 per SEO or Traffic & Market Toolkit sale; $50–$100 for other toolkits; $10 per trial. 120-day cookie. **[third-party, consistent across sources]** — [affiliatepicker](https://affiliatepicker.com/programs/semrush/); [bloggerspassion](https://bloggerspassion.com/semrush-affiliate-program-review/)
- **Shopify**: pays 100% of each of the first two payments (said elsewhere to be a 200% bounty), or $2,000 flat for Shopify Plus. **[third-party, unconfirmed]** — [findstack](https://findstack.com/resources/best-affiliate-programs)
- **BigCommerce**: 200% bounty ($60–$1,500, depending on plan). **[third-party]** — [findstack](https://findstack.com/resources/best-affiliate-programs)
- **Wix**: $100 per Premium sale. **[third-party]**; official page: [Wix affiliates](https://www.wix.com/about/affiliates)
- **Squarespace**: commission per purchase; the rate was not found. — [findstack](https://findstack.com/resources/best-affiliate-programs)
- **WP Engine**: $200 flat per sale (or 100% of the first month), run through ShareASale. **[third-party]** — [affiliatepicker](https://affiliatepicker.com/programs/wp-engine/)
- **Hostinger**: 60% of each sale, one-time. **[third-party]** — [getlasso](https://getlasso.co/niche/web-hosting/)
- **Elementor**: 50% per sale; $70 per sale for Cloud Websites. **[third-party]** — [onlinemediamasters](https://onlinemediamasters.com/wordpress-affiliate-marketing-programs/)
- **FreshBooks**: up to $10 per trial and up to $200 per paid upgrade. 120-day cookie, $50 minimum payout, paid monthly. **[official/third-party]** — [FreshBooks Affiliate Program](https://www.freshbooks.com/affiliate-program)
- **QuickBooks**: Business Affiliate Program pays per subscription; Canada shows "up to $250." **[official-domain snippet]** — [QuickBooks affiliates](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/partners/qbbusinessaffiliates/)
- **Housecall Pro**: $320 one-time, 30-day cookie, paid after the referral has been on the platform for 30+ days. **[third-party; official page exists]** — [openaffiliate](https://openaffiliate.dev/programs/housecall-pro); [Housecall Pro paid affiliates](https://www.housecallpro.com/paid-affiliates/)
- **ServiceTitan**: $500 when the referral attends a demo, and up to $10,000 if they sign up. **[official-domain snippet]** — [ServiceTitan Referrals](https://www.servicetitan.com/referrals)
- **ClickUp**: $24 per referral, 90-day cookie. **[third-party, unconfirmed]** — [supademo](https://supademo.com/blog/saas-affiliate-programs)
- **Canva**: about $29.85–$36 one-time per Pro subscription, 30-day cookie. **[third-party, unconfirmed]** — [creovateofficial](https://creovateofficial.com/canva-affiliate-program-review-2026.html)
- **Ahrefs**: closed its affiliate program around 2020 and now makes individual sponsorship deals instead. — [Tim Soulo (Ahrefs CMO) on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/timsoulo_why-we-ahrefs-closed-our-affiliate-program-activity-7152513646680510464-zFYX)
- **Zapier**: no public affiliate program. Referral benefits exist only inside the Zapier Experts (consultant) program. — [Zapier community](https://community.zapier.com/show-tell-5/does-zapier-have-an-affiliate-program-13950); [Zapier partners](https://zapier.com/l/partners)

### Inferences
- High-ticket bounties (ServiceTitan up to $10K, Shopify Plus $2K, Housecall Pro $320) pay well once but never recur. For a Google Ads buyer, they are easier to measure against cost per acquisition, but they do not build monthly income.

### Gaps
- None of the bounty amounts for Shopify, BigCommerce, Wix, WP Engine, Hostinger or Elementor were confirmed on official pages in this session.

## Which programs ban PPC or brand bidding? (The user runs Google Ads)

### Takeaway
Every program checked that publishes PPC rules bans **bidding on the brand's own terms**. Some go further. SE Ranking's terms read as a ban on **all paid search placements**. StreamYard and Semrush require **prior approval** for any PPC. Bidding on generic or non-brand keywords is possible only where terms allow it or after written approval.

### Cited Findings
- **SE Ranking**: prohibits "any paid search placement including an advertisement that you purchased through bidding on keywords," and any keywords containing the brand name (e.g. "SE Ranking coupons/discounts/reviews"). **[official-domain snippet]** — [SE Ranking affiliate terms](https://seranking.com/legal/affiliate.html)
- **Webflow**: brand-term bidding is prohibited, including use of the brand in Google Ads keyword targeting. Coupon/deal sites and coupon keyword bidding are also prohibited. **[official-domain snippet]** — [Webflow Affiliate Agreement](https://webflow.com/legal/affiliate-terms); [Webflow help](https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961372613011-Webflow-s-affiliate-program-overview)
- **StreamYard**: no PPC bidding without prior written permission. **[official-domain snippet]** — [StreamYard Affiliate Terms](https://support.streamyard.com/hc/en-us/articles/31889400259092-Affiliate-Terms)
- **Semrush**: all PPC campaigns must be pre-approved. Bidding on Semrush brand terms (including misspellings), direct linking and ad hijacking are prohibited. **[third-party summary of the terms]** — [affiliatepicker](https://affiliatepicker.com/programs/semrush/)

### Inferences
- With SE Ranking, Google Ads that send traffic straight to an affiliate link are likely not allowed. The user would need to run ads to their own content or landing page and ask the affiliate manager whether that is permitted.
- For the other programs, the safe default is to assume brand bidding is banned and to get written approval before running any search ads.

### Gaps
- PPC rules were not found for Kinsta, Cloudways, Demio, Pabbly, Make, Notion, Jasper, Riverside or WebinarJam. Each program's terms need to be checked directly.
