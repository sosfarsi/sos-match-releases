# Recurring Affiliate Programs: Course, Membership, Website and Creator-Business Platforms (as of Oct 2026)

> **Method and confidence note (read first):** Direct page fetching was blocked in this research environment (network egress proxy denied kajabi.com, thinkific.com, teachable.com, podia.com, systeme.io, circle.so, schoolmaker.com, etc.). All findings below come from **web-search result extracts**. Where possible, searches were restricted to the company's own domains (official affiliate pages, help centers, terms), and those are cited as the source. Items cited only to third-party directories/blogs are marked **[SECONDARY - UNCONFIRMED]**. Search-extract summaries can mis-read pages (e.g., monthly vs annual prices), so every figure here should be spot-checked on the live page before publishing or spending ad money. Today's date: 2026-10-07.
>
> **Calculation convention:** "Est. $/mo per customer" = commission % x the mid-tier plan's **monthly-billing** price. "Months to $100-150" = $100 / monthly commission to $150 / monthly commission, rounded. Annual-billing customers usually yield a lower effective monthly figure (paid up front once a year).

## Which pay 25%+ recurring for the customer's lifetime, today?

### Takeaway
On the evidence available (official-domain search extracts), the programs that currently advertise **25%+ recurring for the customer's lifetime** are: **Thinkific (30%)**, **Systeme.io (60%)**, **Mighty Networks (30% on Partner Program page; agreement says 20%)**, **Skool (40%)**, **LearnWorlds (30%)**, **ClickFunnels (30%)**, **Leadpages (30%)**, **Builderall (30%, conditional on staying "active")** and possibly **Searchie/Membership.io (30%, lifetime not confirmed)**. Kajabi, Circle, Stan, Podia and Simplero pay below 25% or are capped; Teachable, Kartra, Podia, Simplero and SamCart cap the duration (12/12/12/18/24 months).

- Stan Store at the entry Creator plan (~$5.80/mo) takes ~1.5-2 years per customer to reach $100.

## Which have changed terms in 2024-2026?

### Takeaway
**Kajabi** is the clear 2025 change: from a flat 30% (lifetime while active) to a tiered model (one-time bounties for first 4, then 10-20%) starting **May 2025**, with legacy 30% partners grandfathered **conditionally**. **Teachable** has moved from lifetime to a 12-month cap (date not confirmed). Other likely changes: Kartra (now 12 months, previously marketed "for life"), Simplero (now 18 months; older sources say 25% lifetime), Circle (now $100 + tiered 10-20%), Leadpages (PartnerStack migration + bonuses), ThriveCart (added subscription plans). **No official evidence found that Thinkific moved from lifetime to limited** — its own page still says lifetime as of these searches.

### Inferences
- Kajabi existing affiliates are only partially grandfathered; anyone who stops producing new referrals for 12 months loses legacy 30%.
- Pattern across the category: shift from flat lifetime % to tiered/volume-based or time-capped models (Kajabi, Circle, Kartra, Simplero, Teachable). Lifetime holdouts are Thinkific, Systeme, Skool, Mighty, LearnWorlds, ClickFunnels, Leadpages.

## Which explicitly ban or restrict PPC/brand bidding?

### Takeaway
Explicit brand-bidding bans were found (on official-domain sources) for **Kajabi, Mighty Networks, Circle, LearnWorlds, ClickFunnels, Kartra, Leadpages and Systeme.io**. **Mighty Networks** is the most restrictive (also forbids outbidding Mighty in positions 1-5 on *any* term, zero-tolerance forfeiture). **Kajabi** also forbids linking ads directly to Kajabi or including the partner link in ads. Non-brand Google Ads to your own landing page are generally allowed (explicitly so for Kajabi). For Thinkific, Teachable, Podia, Skool, Simplero, SamCart, ThriveCart, Stan, Builderall and Searchie no official PPC rule was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Kajabi:** cannot use "Kajabi" in keywords ("Kajabi courses" not allowed; "online course builder" allowed); cannot link ads directly to Kajabi-owned sites or include the partner link in the ad; permitted: paid traffic to your own page (e.g., "How to choose the right online course platform") that contains the partner link — [Kajabi Partner Terms and Services FAQs](https://help.kajabi.com/en/articles/17175741-partner-terms-and-services-faqs)
- **Mighty Networks:** may not bid on restricted terms incl. "Mighty Networks", "...coupon", "...discount", variations/misspellings on Google, Bing, Yahoo, Facebook etc.; may not bid to appear higher than Mighty for **any** search term in positions 1-5; must exclude trademarks in automated campaigns; violation = forfeiture of at least past 30 days' fees, balance set to $0 without warning, possible termination — [Mighty Partner Program T&Cs](https://affiliate.mightynetworks.com/terms); [Affiliate Program Agreement](https://www.mightynetworks.com/affiliate-program-agreement)
- **Circle:** prohibits bidding on any Restricted Terms (incl. variations/misspellings) on Google, Bing, MSN, Yahoo, Facebook or other networks; bans cookie stuffing and URL masking — [Circle Affiliate Terms and Conditions](https://circle.so/affiliate-terms-and-conditions)
- **LearnWorlds:** no bidding on "LearnWorlds" or variations/misspellings in PPC/PPI without prior approval; can't use LearnWorlds as display URL or direct-link; no brand in domains; violations can cancel outstanding commissions — [LearnWorlds Affiliate Program T&Cs](https://www.learnworlds.com/affiliate-program-terms-conditions/)
- **ClickFunnels:** bidding on trademarked terms = immediate removal; no paid-search techniques based on ClickFunnels trademarks *or competitors' trademarks*; no "ClickFunnels" in URLs/domains; long list of protected marks — [ClickFunnels Affiliate Agreement](https://app.clickfunnels.com/affiliate-agreement); [ClickFunnels Affiliate Terms](https://signup.clickfunnels.com/affiliate-terms)
- **Kartra:** must not bid on keywords including Kartra trademarks or domain names; exception for bidding solely in a non-English language — [Kartra Affiliate Agreement](https://kartra.com/affiliateagreement/)
- **Leadpages:** paid ads on Google/Facebook/YouTube allowed, but cannot bid on Leadpages branded keywords — [Leadpages Support: Affiliate Partner Program](https://support.leadpages.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009044771-Leadpages-Affiliate-Partner-Program); [Leadpages Affiliates](https://leadpages.com/affiliates)
- **Systeme.io:** "no paid ads bidding on [the] brand name" — [Systeme Help: How the affiliate program works](https://help.systeme.io/article/162-how-the-systeme-io-affiliate-program-works) (extract may come from systeme's guidance on creating *your own* program; moderate confidence)
- **Thinkific:** "PPC bidding on brand keywords is usually prohibited" — [SECONDARY - UNCONFIRMED](https://affiliatejob.org/programs/thinkific)
- **Teachable:** the only PPC/trademark language surfaced came from individual Teachable-hosted schools' terms, not Teachable's own partner terms — not usable; Teachable Creator Referral T&Cs exist at [teachable.com/creator-referrals-terms-and-conditions](https://www.teachable.com/creator-referrals-terms-and-conditions) but content was not extracted.
- **Podia:** no PPC rule found; prohibits self-referral and cash-back/commission sharing — [Podia Help](https://help.podia.com/en/articles/11370941-becoming-a-podia-affiliate)

### Inferences
- For a Google Ads-driven affiliate, the compliant play across nearly all programs is non-brand, category/intent keywords ("best online course platform", "Kajabi alternatives" may still be risky since it contains the brand) sending traffic to the affiliate's own comparison/review page, not direct-linking.
- "X vs Y" or "X alternative" keywords contain trademarks and likely violate Kajabi, Mighty, Circle, LearnWorlds, ClickFunnels (which also bans competitors' trademarks), Kartra and Leadpages rules.
- Mighty's positions 1-5 rule means even non-brand campaigns could breach terms if they outrank Mighty's own ads.

### Gaps
- Official PPC policies not found for Thinkific, Teachable, Podia, Skool, Simplero, Searchie/Membership.io, SamCart, ThriveCart, Stan Store, Builderall — must be checked in each program's dashboard/terms (often PartnerStack or Impact program terms) before running ads.
- PartnerStack/Impact listing pages could not be accessed.
