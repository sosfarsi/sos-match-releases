# Broad-Demand Business Software: Recurring Affiliate Commissions (20%+, ideally lifetime)

Research date: 2026-10-07. Method: Network egress proxy BLOCKED direct WebFetch of vendor domains tried (partners.livechat.com, text.com, help.tidio.com), so every fact below comes from WebSearch result summaries. Verification labels:
- **[V2-official]** = search-engine summary of the vendor's own page (official URL in results; page not opened directly).
- **[V3-secondary]** = third-party directory or blog (Reditus, UpPromote, Referly, RevShare, FlexOffers, affililist, aistudios, customgpt, eesel, websiterating, linkwhisper, etc.). Treat as unconfirmed; these often go out of date.
- Nothing in these notes was confirmed by opening a vendor page in full. Re-check the official pages before publishing.

## Q1: Which programs pay recurring LIFETIME commission of 20%+ (the target profile)?

### Takeaway
Only a few general-purpose tools still advertise **lifetime** recurring 20%+. The best confirmed fit is **Text.com (formerly LiveChat): 20% lifetime, rising to 22%, with a 120-day cookie**. **Pictory** (20–50% tiered, described as lifetime) and **Tidio** (30% lifetime historically, now possibly restructured) are possible fits but need checking. **pCloud** (20% recurring on subscriptions) and **Typeform** (20% recurring "every month the referral stays paid") also appear lifetime-like, but this rests on secondary sources only. Most large-brand tools have moved to **12-month caps**.

### Cited Findings
**Text.com / LiveChat (live chat, helpdesk, chatbot; products: Text App, LiveChat, HelpDesk, ChatBot)**
- At least 20% commission for the lifetime of every paid Text App, LiveChat, HelpDesk or ChatBot subscription. Recurring and lifetime on every payment, climbing to 22% after 5 paying referrals [V2-official]. — [Text Partner Program](https://partners.livechat.com/ecommerce-partners); [Affiliate Program](https://partners.livechat.com/affiliate-program/); [text.com blog](https://www.text.com/blog/ai-affiliate-programs/)
- 120-day cookie [V2-official/V3]. — [text.com blog](https://www.text.com/blog/ai-affiliate-programs/)
- **Conflict or ambiguity:** One search summary says there are two tracks. The "Solution Program" (you manage client licenses) pays recurring revenue or discounts, while the "Affiliate Program" is described as "earn a one-time commission per referral." Other snippets say the affiliate track is "5–22% recurring." Check partners.livechat.com/affiliate-program directly before relying on "lifetime" for the pure-affiliate track. — [Partner program pages](https://partners.livechat.com/shopify-partners/)
- Rebrand: The program is now called the "Text Partner Program" on partners.livechat.com [V2-official]. — [Text Partner Program](https://partners.livechat.com/ecommerce-partners)
- Plan price: not retrieved in this pass (gap).

**Pictory (AI video from text/blogs)**
- Tiered recurring commission: Standard 20%, VIP (50 customers) up to 30%, Super (250) 40%, Mega (500) 50%. Also a free lifetime Premium account and a $1,000 cash bonus. 90-day cookie, $10 minimum, PayPal (bank transfer for Mega), runs on FirstPromoter [V3-secondary]. — [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-program-directory/pictory/); [Pictory partner page](https://pictory.ai/?p=4567)
- Secondary summaries call it lifetime recurring. I did not see the word "lifetime" for commissions on an official page (the official snippet says "recurring commissions of up to 50%"). — [Pictory](https://pictory.ai/?p=4567)

**Tidio (live chat and AI chatbot for SMB/ecommerce)**
- 30% lifetime recurring commissions, per Tidio's own help-center page metadata and secondary sources [V2/V3]. — [Tidio Help Center](https://help.tidio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5444003167388-Affiliate-Program); [RevShare](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/tidio)
- **Conflict:** A secondary source reports the PartnerStack marketplace listing now shows 20–25% recurring **for two years**, which would mean the program was restructured. — [PartnerStack Tidio](https://market.partnerstack.com/page/tidiollc); [Reditus](https://getreditus.com/affiliate-programs/tidio)

**pCloud (cloud storage)**
- 20% of each monthly or annual payment on subscription plans (recurring). 20% one-time on Lifetime plans. 45-day cookie [V3-secondary, citing pCloud's own affiliate page]. — [pCloud affiliate](https://pcdn-www.pcloud.com/affiliate); [pCloud help](https://help.pcloud.com/topics/referral-affiliate-promotions)
- Caveat: Consumer pricing is far below $50/mo, and pCloud pushes one-time "Lifetime" plans, which pay only once.

**Typeform (forms and surveys)**
- 20% recurring, paid "every month your referral maintains a paid subscription." Monthly PayPal payouts [V3-secondary]. — [Post Affiliate Pro directory](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/typeform-affiliate-program/); [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/typeform/)
- No duration cap was found, but this is unconfirmed on an official page.

**VPN / security (renewal commissions = effectively lifetime)**
- NordVPN: 100% revenue share on 1-month plans, 40% on 1- and 2-year plans, and **30% on all recurring (renewal) sales** [V3-secondary]. — [UpPromote NordVPN](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-program-directory/nordvpn/); [Authority Hacker](https://authorityhacker.com/vpn-affiliate-programs/)
- Surfshark: 40% on new subscriptions. Renewal commissions are reported as 20–35% (sources conflict, one says "50% for renewals") [V3-secondary, conflicting]. — [LinkWhisper](https://linkwhisper.com/vpn-affiliate-programs/)
- NordPass: 30% on new sign-ups, **10% on renewals** (B2C) [V2-official]. — [NordPass affiliate](https://nordpass.com/affiliate/)
- Caveat: These are consumer products priced at a few dollars per month, far below the $50–100/mo target.

### Inferences
- Among general business tools with a strong company behind them, **Text.com/LiveChat** is the only program here that clearly matches "20%+ lifetime" and is stated on the vendor's own domain. Its plans fall in the mid-market range (price not verified in this pass).
- Tidio and Pictory may still pay lifetime commissions but recently changed or show conflicting terms, so treat them as "verify before featuring."
- VPN programs pay on renewals but fail the $50–100+/mo price criterion.

### Gaps
- Could not open partners.livechat.com, text.com or help.tidio.com (blocked by the proxy), so lifetime terms there are unconfirmed beyond search snippets.
- 1Password and Backblaze affiliate terms were not found.
- PPC/brand-bidding rules were not found for any program in this group.

## Q2: Which big-brand programs pay 20%+ recurring but LIMITED (12–24 months)?

### Takeaway
Most high-demand SaaS with strong brands cap commissions at 12 months: Pipedrive (20–30%), Jotform (30%), Fireflies (10–30%), ElevenLabs (22%), Jasper (25%), Synthesia (25%), Webflow (50%), Zoho (15–20%), Keap (20–25%), Quo/OpenPhone (20%). Murf runs 24 months. HeyGen cut to 3 months.

### Cited Findings
**CRM**
- **Pipedrive** (sales CRM): Tier 3 pays 20% revenue share for the first 12 months, Tier 2 pays 30%, and Tier 1 has custom terms. 90-day cookie. Paid monthly via PartnerStack on the 13th with a $5 minimum. Add-ons count during year one [V2-official]. — [Pipedrive affiliate terms](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/affiliate-program-terms); [PartnerStack](https://market.partnerstack.com/page/pipedrive)
- **Zoho** (CRM, Books, One suite): 15% of revenue Zoho receives in the 12 months after the sale, if the customer stays at least 60 days. Tiers are 15%, 18% (Super) and 20% (Elite). 90-day cookie. Maximum $25,000 commission per deal [V2-official]. — [Zoho Affiliate FAQ](https://www.zoho.com/affiliate/faq.html); [Zoho Affiliate](https://zoho.com/affiliate)
- **Keap**: 20–25% recurring in the first year [V3-secondary comparison table]. — [UpPromote Zoho page (comparison)](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-program-directory/zoho)
- **monday.com / monday CRM**: "Up to 100% commission on the first year's sales" under a tier model. 90-day cookie that resets on re-click. Monthly PayPal/Stripe payouts [V2-official]. A secondary source says the PartnerStack listing shows "up to 20% per closed deal" for partners and CPL deals for online affiliates, so actual terms depend on the partner group. — [monday.com affiliate](https://monday.com/affiliate-program); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/monday-com-affiliate-program)
- **Bitrix24**: No classic affiliate program. Instead a reseller partner program with 20/40/50% purchase discounts by level, plus service revenue. Not lifetime affiliate [V2-official]. — [Bitrix24 Partners](https://partners.bitrix24.com/); [Partner policy PDF](https://partners.bitrix24.com/private/partner-policy.pdf)

**Business phone**
- **Quo (formerly OpenPhone)**: 20% on referred customers for the first 12 months from their start date. Paid the month after earning via PayPal or Stripe, on PartnerStack [V2-official]. Older OpenPhone pages said commissions could last "the lifetime of the account unless otherwise stated." That wording is older and now superseded by the 12-month terms. — [Quo partner program](https://support.quo.com/community/partner-program); [PartnerStack Quo](https://market.partnerstack.com/page/quo); [old OpenPhone page](https://support.openphone.com/community/partner-program)

**AI tools**
- **ElevenLabs** (AI voice): 22% for the first 12 months on Starter, Creator, Pro and Scale plans. 11% on the Business plan. No enterprise payouts. 90-day cookie, $5 minimum. Commission releases after the license has been active more than 90 days. On PartnerStack [V2-official]. — [ElevenLabs affiliates](https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates)
- **Jasper** (AI writing): 25% recurring for the first 12 months on Creator and Pro plans [V3-secondary]. — [eesel.ai](https://eesel.ai/blog/ai-affiliate-programs); [CustomGPT](https://customgpt.ai/ai-tools-have-affiliate-programs/)
- **Synthesia** (AI avatar video): 25% on sales for the first year, 60-day cookie [V3-secondary]. — [CustomGPT](https://customgpt.ai/ai-tools-have-affiliate-programs/)
- **HeyGen** (AI avatar video): Now 35% recurring for **3 months only** (Social Creator Program). A promotional 50% rate ran Aug 1 to Oct 31 [2026 per snippet]. 30-day cookie, PayPal payouts. Payout minimum conflicts ($100 vs $30) [V2/V3]. The older "20% recurring for 12 months" is outdated. — [HeyGen affiliate](https://www.heygen.com/affiliate-program); [Reditus](https://getreditus.com/affiliate-programs/heygen)
- **Murf AI** (AI voice): 20% for 24 months, 90-day cookie [V3-secondary]. — [Tapfiliate blog](https://tapfiliate.com/blog/best-ai-affiliate-programs-gp/); [RevShare Murf](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/murf-ai)
- **Fireflies.ai** (meeting notes): 10–30% recurring for 12 months, with the rate rising as referred MRR grows. 90-day cookie. Quarterly PayPal payouts, $50 minimum [V2-official]. — [Fireflies affiliate](https://fireflies.ai/affiliate)

**Website builders / hosting**
- **Webflow**: 50% recurring for the first 12 months, 90-day cookie [V3-secondary]. — [UpPromote Webflow](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-program-directory/webflow); [WebsiteRating](https://websiterating.com/blog/website-builders/best-website-builder-affiliate-programs)
- **Hostinger**: 40%+ of the **initial** purchase only, which covers up to 12 months prepaid. No renewal commissions. 30-day cookie [V3-secondary]. — [BloggingTips](https://www.bloggingtips.com/affiliate-programs/hostinger)
- **Duda**: 20% on sales, 90-day cookie. Duration not stated [V3-secondary]. — [WebsiteRating](https://websiterating.com/blog/website-builders/best-website-builder-affiliate-programs)

**Forms**
- **Jotform**: 30% recurring for one year on new paid users [V2-official]. — [Jotform affiliate](https://jotform.com/partnership/affiliate); [Jotform blog](https://www.jotform.com/blog/introducing-jotform-affiliate-program/)

### Inferences
- The "huge demand plus strong company" brands (Pipedrive, Zoho, monday, ElevenLabs, Webflow, Jotform, Fireflies) almost all cap at 12 months. A 12-month 20–30% on a $50–100/mo plan works out to roughly $120–360 per customer.
- Monday.com's "up to 100% of first year" may beat 12-month recurring on value, but terms vary by partner group.

### Gaps
- Plan prices per month were not verified in this pass for any vendor.
- Synthesia and Jasper terms come only from secondary listicles.
- InVideo, Otter.ai and Gorgias commission terms were not found.

## Q3: Which categories pay flat bounties instead of recurring (poor fit for the lifetime goal)?

### Takeaway
Business phone (except Quo), HR/payroll and e-signature (SignNow) mostly pay flat CPA or CPL rather than recurring percentages.

### Cited Findings
- **RingCentral**: Up to $25 per lead plus up to $60 per sale ($85 max), paid every 30 days [V2-official blog; date not confirmed, may be older]. — [RingCentral blog](https://www.ringcentral.com/us/en/blog/ringcentrals-affiliate-program/)
- **Nextiva**: $100 per phone line, up to $300 per AI sale, 90-day cookie, monthly via PartnerStack [V2-official]. — [Nextiva affiliates](https://www.nextiva.com/affiliates)
- **Aircall**: $75 per qualified lead (pay-per-lead) [V3-secondary]. — [affililist](https://www.affililist.com/tag/businessphone)
- **Dialpad, Grasshopper**: No terms found.
- **Deel**: $500 per sales-qualified referral plus $1,000 per new paying customer ($1,500 total) [V2-official]. — [Deel affiliates](https://www.deel.com/partner/affiliates/)
- **Gusto**: Flat per-customer bounty. Reports conflict ($200+ per customer vs "up to $1,000," $300 per payroll-running customer plus a $200 bonus) [V3-secondary, conflicting]. — [PartnerStack Gusto](https://market.partnerstack.com/page/gusto); [UpPromote Gusto](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/gusto/)
- **SignNow (airSlate)**: $40–80 CPA by monthly volume, up to $1,000 for API deals, 60-day cookie [V2/V3]. — [SignNow blog](https://www.signnow.com/blog/signnow-affiliate-program/); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/signnow-affiliate-program/)

### Inferences
- Deel's $1,500 bounty can beat years of 20% recurring on a $50/mo plan, but it is not recurring.
- No lifetime-recurring program was found in phone/VoIP, HR/payroll or e-signature.

### Gaps
- DocuSign, PandaDoc, FreshBooks, Xero, Wave, Freshdesk/Freshworks, Gorgias, Backblaze, 1Password, Otter, InVideo, Dialpad and Grasshopper: no affiliate terms retrieved in this pass.
- Company size indicators (users, funding, public listing) were not gathered with sources in this pass. Do not assert them without verification.
- Brand-bidding / PPC rules were not surfaced in any search summary. They usually sit in program T&Cs (e.g., pipedrive.com/en/affiliate-program-terms) and need a direct read.

### Summary ranking (lifetime fit x strong demand)
1. Text.com / LiveChat: 20–22% lifetime, 120-day cookie [V2-official, needs a check on affiliate vs solution track].
2. Tidio: 30% lifetime historically, possibly now 20–25% for 2 years [conflicting].
3. Pictory: 20–50% tiered recurring, reportedly lifetime [V3].
4. Typeform: 20% recurring, no cap stated [V3].
5. pCloud: 20% recurring on subscriptions [V3]; NordVPN: 30% on renewals [V3]. Both are consumer-priced.
Next best (12–24 months): Webflow 50%/12mo, Pipedrive 20–30%/12mo, Jotform 30%/12mo, ElevenLabs 22%/12mo, Jasper and Synthesia 25%/12mo, Murf 20%/24mo, Quo 20%/12mo, Zoho 15–20%/12mo, monday up to 100% of year one.
