# Official Platform-Partner Tools (Google, TikTok, Shopify, etc.) With Recurring Affiliate Commissions

Research date: 2026-10-07. Method note: the network egress proxy BLOCKED direct fetches of vendor domains (metricool.com and omnisend.com both returned EGRESS_BLOCKED), so almost every fact below comes from WebSearch result snippets, many of which are summaries of the vendor's own pages. Verification levels used:
- **[V-snippet-official]** = search snippet drawn from the vendor's own domain (official page, but not opened in full by me)
- **[V-secondary]** = third-party aggregator / directory / press (getlasso, uppromote, affpaying, contentstudio, Marketing Dive, etc.)
- **[Unverified]** = not confirmed; listed only as a gap

Nothing here was read as a full page, so treat exact numbers as "very likely current, confirm on the vendor page before publishing."

## Which officially-partnered, high-demand tools pay 25%+ recurring, ideally lifetime, on plans ~$100+/mo?

### Takeaway
Only a handful clear the "25%+ recurring, lifetime" bar: **Localo** (25% lifetime, Google Business Profile tool), **Metricool** (25% lifetime, rising to 50% after 200 paid referrals, but with a per-user commission cap), **AdCreative.ai** (30% lifetime, 35–40% tiers), and **Publer** (20% lifetime rising to 25–30% by volume). The big "badged" enterprise names (Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Later, Agorapulse, Madgicx, Triple Whale, Omnisend, Gorgias) either cap the term at 6–24 months or pay under 25%.

### Inferences
- **Commission math per $100/mo of plan price** (arithmetic only, since plan prices could not be verified because the vendor sites were blocked):
  - 50% (Metricool Tier 2) → $50/mo → reaches $100–150 in 2–3 months, but the commission stops at the $300/user cap (about 6 months at $100/mo).
  - 30% (AdCreative.ai base; Publer top tier) → $30/mo → 4–5 months to reach $100–150.
  - 25% (Localo; Metricool Tier 1) → $25/mo → 4–6 months. Metricool Tier 1 stops at $100/user total (if "up to $100 per user" is a lifetime cap).
  - 20% (SocialBee, Omnisend, Publer base) → $20/mo → 5–8 months.
  - 15% (BrightLocal) → $15/mo → 7–10 months.
- **Localo's top plan** is implied to be about $499/mo, because $124.75 ÷ 25% = $499. On that plan a referral earns $124.75/mo, which reaches ~$100–150 in **about 1 month**, for life. This is derived from the vendor's own figure, not a fetched pricing page.
- **Metricool's "up to $X per user" looks like a lifetime cap** per referred user, which would make it far less "lifetime" than it is marketed. This needs confirming on the full page. If it is a lifetime cap, Metricool's real ceiling is $100 per user (Tier 1) or $300 per user (Tier 2).
- **Lifetime 25%+ is not offered by the tools with confirmed official badges.** The tools with verified badges (Hootsuite, Sprout, Later on TikTok; Madgicx as a Google Premier Partner; Omnisend as Shopify Plus Certified) pay 15–20%, mostly time-limited. The tools paying 25%+ lifetime (Localo, AdCreative.ai, Publer tiers, Metricool) have no official badge confirmed in this pass.
- **Best fit for "official partner + 25%+ lifetime + ~$100+/mo plan":** no candidate fully verified. The closest are Localo (strong lifetime terms, high-priced top plan, GBP-focused, no Google Partner badge found) and AdCreative.ai (30–40% lifetime, partner badge unverified).

### Gaps
- **Plan prices (per month)** for every tool: the vendor pricing pages were blocked by the proxy and were not otherwise verified. The only exception is Localo's implied $499 top tier.
- **Paid-search / brand-bidding rules**: not found for any program. These are usually in the network terms (PartnerStack, Impact, ShareASale), which were not reachable.
- **Official partner status not checked for**: Metricool, SocialBee, Publer, Vista Social, Sendible, Agorapulse, AdCreative.ai, Localo, BrightLocal and Optmyzr (Google Partners / TikTok / LinkedIn / Pinterest directories were not fetched). TikTok's current directory (ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/marketing-partners) was not opened.
- **Not researched (ran out of the tool-call budget)**: Buffer, Planable, Bïrch/Revealbot, Smartly, Birdeye, Podium, Yext, Klaviyo, Postscript (real SMS program), Attentive, Shopify, Wix, Canva, and the LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, Microsoft and Amazon partner directories. From general knowledge, Klaviyo, Smartly, Birdeye, Podium and Yext mainly run agency/referral partner programs rather than public % recurring affiliate programs, but this is unverified.
- **Size indicators** (customers, funding) are missing for most tools. The only one found is Localo's 26,815+ GBPs.
- **Sprout Social terms conflict** (20% vs a "$1,500 per order" snippet), and Agorapulse terms conflict (15% first-year vs "20% recurring"). Both are unresolved.
- **Hootsuite, Later and Sprout terms** come only from a 2026 competitor blog (ContentStudio) and are not confirmed on the vendors' own pages.
