# Platform Strength, Demand and Competitors (HighLevel, Skool, ManyChat, ClickFunnels, Kajabi, Thinkific, Mighty Networks, Systeme.io, Kartra, Keap, Vendasta)

Research date: 2026-10-07. Method: web search snippets (WebSearch). Direct fetches of vendor/press-wire pages were mostly not attempted or were blocked (prnewswire.com returned EGRESS_BLOCKED). Verification labels used below:
- **[Primary]** = company press release, investor filing or official company page (seen via search result/snippet).
- **[Reputable press]** = Bloomberg, TechCrunch, CTV/CBC, BetaKit, OC Business Journal, Business Wire, etc.
- **[Secondary/estimate]** = data aggregators (GetLatka, Revelio, Tracxn, CB Insights, ZoomInfo) or blogs/review sites. GetLatka figures are often estimates or founder self-reports; treat as rough.
- "Self-reported" = number comes from the company itself and is not independently audited (only Thinkific, a TSX-listed company, publishes audited numbers in this set).

## What does each platform do, in plain language, and who uses it?

### Takeaway
All eleven are "run your online business" software, but they split into three groups: agency/local-business marketing systems (HighLevel, Vendasta, Keap), course/community/creator platforms (Skool, Kajabi, Thinkific, Mighty Networks), funnel/all-in-one sales-page builders (ClickFunnels, Systeme.io, Kartra), plus one chat-automation specialist (ManyChat).

### Cited Findings
- **HighLevel (confirmed website: gohighlevel.com; affiliate portal at affiliate.gohighlevel.com, help center at help.gohighlevel.com)**: all-in-one CRM/marketing platform aimed at marketing agencies, capturing leads via SMS, email, voicemail, Facebook Messenger, plus websites, funnels and calendars; offers white-label and "SaaS Mode" so agencies can resell it under their own brand with Stripe billing their clients — [HighPerformr company summary, Secondary](https://www.highperformr.ai/company/highlevel); [HighLevel Affiliate Program, Primary](https://affiliate.gohighlevel.com/); [HighLevel Support Portal, Primary](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001174612-5-ways-to-find-your-first-5-affiliate-referrals); [FitSmallBusiness white-label CRM roundup, Secondary](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/best-white-label-crm/)
- **Skool**: community-plus-course platform; flat-price subscription per community (Hobby $9/mo, Pro $99/mo); community owners keep member subscription revenue minus a transaction fee (Hobby 10% + $0.30; Pro 2.9% + $0.30) — [Ruzuku Skool pricing, Secondary](https://www.ruzuku.com/compare/skool-pricing); [Taskade Skool history, Secondary](https://www.taskade.com/blog/skool-sam-ovens-history)
- **ManyChat**: automates marketing/customer conversations on Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger; used by creators, small businesses and brands such as Nike, the New York Times and Yahoo (per TechCrunch) — [ManyChat blog, Primary](https://manychat.com/blog/manychat-raises-140m/); [TechCrunch, Reputable press](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2996937)
- **ClickFunnels**: sales-funnel/landing-page builder for online sellers, founded 2014 by Russell Brunson and Todd Dickerson — [Founder Reports interview, Secondary](https://founderreports.com/interview/clickfunnels/); [ClickFunnels blog, Primary](https://www.clickfunnels.com/blog/how-clickfunnels-grew-into-a-million-dollar-business/)
- **Kajabi**: lets coaches/creators build websites and sell courses, memberships, communities and podcasts — [OC Business Journal, May 2021, Reputable press](https://www.ocbj.com/news/2021/may/10/kajabi-nabs-550m-knowledge-economy/)
- **Thinkific**: online course platform, publicly listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX: THNC); also has an enterprise tier "Thinkific Plus" — [Thinkific investor release, Primary](https://investors.thinkific.com/2026-03-05-Thinkific-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Financial-Results)
- **Mighty Networks**: community + course + events + membership platform for creators/brands; founded 2010 by Gina Bianchini (previously co-founded Ning) and others — [Wikipedia: Gina Bianchini, Secondary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gina_Bianchini); [Tracxn, Secondary](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/mighty-networks/__lRkhKhtsNP5VpRGaFQZGyIyuTYJyiK-VH-WC_75RheU)
- **Systeme.io**: low-cost all-in-one (funnels, email, courses) for solopreneurs; French founder Aurélien Amacker; bootstrapped — [Mixergy interview, Secondary](https://mixergy.com/interviews/systeme-io-with-aurelien-amacker/); [GetLatka, Secondary/estimate](https://getlatka.com/companies/systemeio)
- **Kartra**: all-in-one funnels/email/membership platform launched 2018 by Genesis Digital (makers of WebinarJam/EverWebinar); co-founders Mike Filsaime and Andy Jenkins (Jenkins died 2021) — [DropInBlog, Secondary](https://dropinblog.com/blog/what-is-kartra/); [Ryan Coisson review, Secondary](https://www.ryancoisson.com/751/kartra-review-and-buyers-guide/)
- **Keap (formerly Infusionsoft)**: small-business CRM + email/marketing automation; now owned by Thryv Holdings — [Thryv press release, Primary](https://www.thryv.com/news/thryv-holdings-announces-strategic-acquisition-of-keap/); [Wikipedia: Keap, Secondary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keap)
- **Vendasta**: white-label platform/marketplace for agencies and media companies selling digital services to local businesses; 250+ resellable products plus in-house fulfillment (SEO, listings, reputation, websites); based in Saskatoon, Canada — [Affpaying / Vendasta program summary, Secondary](https://www.affpaying.com/vendastaaffiliateprogram); [CTV News, Reputable press](https://www.ctvnews.ca/saskatoon/article/vendasta-cuts-20-jobs-in-saskatoon-as-company-pivots-to-ai/)

### Inferences
- For an affiliate marketer, HighLevel and Vendasta sell to agencies (higher-ticket B2B), while Skool/Kajabi/Thinkific/Mighty/Systeme/ClickFunnels/Kartra sell to coaches and creators; ManyChat sells to anyone using Instagram/Meta messaging.

### Gaps
- Official "About" pages were not fetched directly (proxy restrictions/time budget); descriptions rely on press and secondary summaries.

## Direct competitors for HighLevel and ManyChat, and their affiliate programs

### Takeaway
HighLevel's closest rivals (agency/white-label CRM) are Vendasta, DashClicks, Keap, Kartra/ClickFunnels (partial overlap); several match its 40% recurring commission. ManyChat's rivals (Chatfuel, SendPulse, Tidio and newer Instagram DM tools) mostly pay lower rates (20-25%) than ManyChat's tiered 30-50%.

### Cited Findings
**Affiliate programs of the target platforms (for reference)**
- HighLevel: 40% recurring for life of the account — [HighLevel Affiliate, Primary](https://affiliate.gohighlevel.com/)
- ManyChat: 30% rising to 40% after 30 sales and 50% after 200 sales, for first 12 months — [UpPromote directory, Secondary](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/manychat/)
- Skool: 40% recurring — [Ruzuku, Secondary](https://www.ruzuku.com/compare/skool-pricing)
- Systeme.io: 40% recurring, lifetime cookie; Thinkific 30% recurring (90-day cookie); Mighty Networks 20-30% recurring (sources conflict); Kajabi: conflicting reports (20% vs 30% lifetime; one source describes 100% bounty for first 4 referrals then 10-20%) — [SchoolMaker Kajabi review, Secondary](https://www.schoolmaker.com/blog/kajabi-affiliate-program); [SchoolMaker Mighty review, Secondary](https://www.schoolmaker.com/blog/mighty-networks-affiliate-program); [EarnifyHub, Secondary](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/affiliate/kajabi-affiliate-program-review-2026). Kajabi's current terms must be checked on its official page.
- Kartra: up to 40% recurring (some say capped at 12 months) plus 10% second tier — [SchoolMaker Kartra review, Secondary](https://schoolmaker.com/blog/kartra-affiliate-program); [PostAffiliatePro, Secondary](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/kartra-affiliate-program/)

**HighLevel competitors**
- Vendasta: 40% recurring on paid accounts, run via PartnerStack ($1.8M affiliate program profits in <2 years per PartnerStack case study) — [Affpaying, Secondary](https://www.affpaying.com/vendastaaffiliateprogram); [PartnerStack, Primary-ish case study](https://partnerstack.com/customers/vendasta)
- DashClicks: rated "best overall white label CRM for agencies" by FitSmallBusiness; affiliate terms not found — [FitSmallBusiness](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/best-white-label-crm/)
- Other white-label CRM roundups — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/best-white-label-crm/); [Perspective](https://www.perspective.co/article/white-label-marketing-software)
- Keap, Kartra, ClickFunnels and Systeme.io overlap with HighLevel for funnels/CRM/email but lack its agency white-label focus (inference from descriptions above).

**ManyChat competitors**
- Chatfuel: 20% recurring for life; SendPulse: 25% recurring for life; Tidio: 25% recurring for life — [UpPromote/SendPulse comparison results, Secondary](https://sendpulse.com/blog/manychat-alternatives); [ChatbotScape, Secondary](https://chatbotscape.com/compare/chatfuel-vs-manychat)
- Other listed alternatives (Instagram-DM tools): see [heyregent](https://heyregent.com/blog/manychat-alternatives), [brandID](https://brandid.app/blog/manychat-alternatives); HighLevel itself also offers Instagram/Facebook DM automation.

### Inferences
- For HighLevel promoters, Vendasta is the most comparable affiliate alternative (same 40% rate) but is a heavier enterprise product with recent layoffs. For ManyChat promoters, competitors pay less, so ManyChat remains the best-paying in its niche, though its commissions are time-limited (12 months) vs. HighLevel's lifetime.

### Gaps
- Affiliate terms for DashClicks, Inro, Spur, UChat, CreatorFlow not found.
- Commission percentages come from affiliate-directory sites and may be outdated; verify on official partner pages before publishing.
