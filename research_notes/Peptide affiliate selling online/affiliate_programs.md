**Method and verification caveat (read first):** Research was done on 2026-10-06. The session's egress proxy blocked direct page fetches (WebFetch returned EGRESS_BLOCKED for fda.gov, finnrick.com, afslaw.com, skool.com, peptideprotocolwiki.com and others). Every figure below therefore comes from **search-engine result snippets** of the URLs cited, observed 2026-10-06. I could not open the pages to confirm the full terms or the "last updated" date. Treat every commission figure as "seen in a search snippet of the cited page on 2026-10-06; primary page not opened." A newcomer should check the terms inside the network dashboard (Impact, Awin, FlexOffers) before joining.

## Q1. Research peptide vendors: which still exist, which have affiliate programs, and on what terms?

### Takeaway
The big US "research use only" (RUO) vendors went through a wave of closures from 2025 to 2026. Amino Asylum went offline after a June 2025 raid. Science.bio closed in January 2026, Peptide Sciences in March 2026, Strate Labs in July 2026 and Eternal Peptides in September 2026. The survivors, such as Swiss Chems, Limitless Life and Core-branded sites, mostly run in-house or Shopify-app affiliate programs (UpPromote, Refersion, iDevAffiliate, dub.co partners) at 10–30% of sales. They are not on mainstream networks; Awin's listing for "RealPeptides" is a rare exception. All of them carry high regulatory risk. The FDA says RUO disclaimers do not protect sellers whose websites show human dosing.

**Enforcement against RUO vendors**
- FDA warning letters to Gram Peptides (Mar 31, 2026) and Wholesale Peptide (Jun 17, 2026) are listed in the FDA warning letter database — [FDA: Gram Peptides 721806](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/gram-peptides-721806-03312026); [FDA: Wholesale Peptide 729447](https://fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/wholesale-peptide-729447-06172026)
- In Aug 2026, FDA CDER sent warning letters to five online peptide vendors: Peak Performance Peptides, Royal Peptides LLC, NuScience Peptides LLC, Peptide Partners LLC, and TXP Innovations LLC (Tex Peptides). The letters covered tesamorelin and ipamorelin as unapproved new drugs. FDA said website dosing information, injection instructions and dose calculators showed the products were meant for human use "despite statements on your product labeling" — [ArentFox Schiff, "RUO-ined"](https://www.afslaw.com/perspectives/alerts/ruo-ined-five-peptide-vendors-learn-research-use-only-not-legal-strategy); [Mondaq/Sheppard Mullin](https://www.sheppard.com/insights/blogs/fda-balances-peptide-policy-with-enforcement-against-unlawful-peptide-sellers)
- More than 50 FDA warning letters had gone to the peptide industry by Sept 2025, and DOJ had moved to criminal guilty pleas against grey-market distributors by late 2025 — [ArentFox Schiff](https://www.afslaw.com/perspectives/alerts/ruo-ined-five-peptide-vendors-learn-research-use-only-not-legal-strategy)
- Regulatory context: on Apr 15, 2026, HHS Secretary Kennedy confirmed that 12 peptides (including BPC-157, TB-500, MOTS-c, injectable GHK-Cu, Melanotan II, Semax) were removed from FDA's Category 2 "do not compound" list. A law firm notes that removal does **not** by itself make them eligible for 503A compounding, and they still cannot be sold as research chemicals for human use — [Frier Levitt](https://www.frierlevitt.com/articles/fda-peptides-do-not-compound-list-update-2026/); [BSCG](https://www.bscg.org/blogs/single/whats-changing-with-peptide-regulation-in-2026). On Jul 23, 2026, an FDA advisory panel backed allowing compounding of BPC-157, KPV, TB-500 and MOTS-c — [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/07/23/fda-panel-okays-peptides-compound-pharmacies-bpc-157-kpv/)

### Inferences
- An affiliate who promotes RUO vendors with human-use framing (dosing, protocols, "results") is producing the kind of content FDA cites as evidence of intended human use. That content raises the risk for the vendor and could raise it for the affiliate. FTC/FDA liability for affiliates is not documented in my sources (see Gaps).
- RUO vendor programs pay well (10–30%, some lifetime) but carry platform risk. When a vendor shuts down, unpaid commissions can vanish, as the reports of Amino Asylum affiliates being owed commissions show. These programs are almost never on mainstream networks, likely because those networks restrict unapproved drugs. That is my inference; I did not verify network policies.
- "Core Peptides" naming is fragmented. corepeptides.com, coreresearchpeptides.com and corepeptidesdirect.com may be separate businesses, so the brand someone means by "Core Peptides" needs confirming.

### Gaps
- Could not open any official affiliate page, so payout thresholds, payout methods, approval criteria and cookie lengths are missing for Swiss Chems, Limitless Life and most others.
- No data on whether Biotech Peptides is still operating or has an affiliate program. One spam-like result titled "Peptide companies are closing affiliate programs" was not usable.
- No primary DOJ press release found for Paradigm Peptides or Amino Asylum.
- Did not find whether FDA warning letters have named affiliates or influencers.

## Q2. Telehealth / compounded peptide programs: which run affiliate programs and on what terms?

### Takeaway
The large telehealth brands (Hims & Hers, Ro, Henry Meds, Hone Health) run normal CPA programs on **Impact** (Hims also on ShareASale, FlexOffers and Katalys). Reported payouts are $10–$75 per new subscriber, from aggregator data. Dedicated peptide telehealth providers (Live Vital, PeRx, Strut, Ways2Well) did not show public affiliate terms. Hims & Hers received FDA warning letters in Sept 2025 over compounded semaglutide claims. Two search hits for "Mochi" and "Eden" affiliate terms belonged to different companies with the same names and should not be used.

### Cited Findings
- **Hims / Hers:** main programs on Impact and ShareASale. Reported $20–$40 per new subscription on Impact with a 30-day cookie, typically a one-time CPA rather than recurring. Payouts Net 30/60 via ACH, check, wire or PayPal, ~$50 minimum (aggregator, unverified) — [commissiondex 2026](https://commissiondex.com/programs/hims/); [LinkClicky](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/hims/); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/hims-inc-affiliate-program/); [Katalys](https://katalys.com/brands/hims-affiliate-program/). Hers is reported at about $20 per sale — [OffersPilot](https://www.offerspilot.com/hers-affiliate-program-review-earn-20-per-sale-promoting-a-trusted-womens-health-brand/)
- **Hims & Hers FDA warning letters (Sept 9, 2025):** FDA cited claims such as "Same active ingredient as Ozempic and Wegovy" and "Clinically proven ingredients" for compounded semaglutide as false or misleading. The letters were part of about 100 sent in a DTC advertising crackdown launched Sept 9, 2025 — [FDA letter, Hims 716567](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/hims-hers-health-inc-dba-hims-716567-09092025); [FDA letter, Hers 716825](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/hims-hers-health-inc-dba-hers-716825-09092025); [Bloomberg Gov](https://news.bgov.com/pharma-and-life-sciences/hims-falls-as-fda-posts-warning-letter-on-semaglutide-claims-1)
- **Ro:** Impact-hosted. Reported "$10, 3%–20% per sale, $5–$10 per lead" (aggregator, undated) — [LinkClicky Ro](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/ro/)
- **Henry Meds:** $25 per subscription purchase on Impact (aggregator) — [LinkClicky Henry Meds](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/henry-meds/); [Refermate](https://refermate.com/store/henry-meds/referral-program)
- **Hone Health** (TRT/men's hormones): $30–$75 CPA, impact.com only — [PrescribedRx telehealth affiliate roundup 2026](https://prescribedrx.com/hone-health-affiliate-program/); [Affplus](https://www.affplus.com/o/hone-health); [VigLink](https://www.viglink.com/merchants/277234/hone-health-affiliate-program)
- **Eden (tryeden, telehealth):** a $300–$400 CPA appeared in one search summary without a clear source page. This is **unverified** and looks unusually high.
- **Mochi Health:** an Affplus offer page and a "Partner With Us" page exist — [Affplus](https://www.affplus.com/o/mochi-health-program); [joinmochi.com/partner-with-us](https://joinmochi.com/partner-with-us). The consumer referral program is "give $40, get $40" — [ReferralCodes](https://referralcodes.com/shop/mochi-health-referral). **Warning:** the "20% of subscription for first year, Php 2,000 threshold" terms in search results come from mochi.ph, a Philippine company, and do **not** apply to Mochi Health — [mochi.ph terms](https://www.mochi.ph/legal-docs/mochi-affiliate-program-terms-and-conditions)
- **Warning:** the "Eden 20–35% recurring, 60-day cookie, PayPal" terms come from eden.so, a different company, not tryeden telehealth — [eden.so](https://eden.so/affiliates/)
- **Peptide-specific telehealth** (Live Vital, PeRx, Strut Health, Ways2Well) offers compounded BPC-157, sermorelin, CJC/ipamorelin and similar. Review sites disclose earning affiliate commissions from some providers, but no public commission terms were found — [HealingMaps](https://healingmaps.com/peptide-telehealth/); [ThisPeptide](https://thispeptide.org/)

### Inferences
- For a newcomer, Impact is the main gateway for mainstream telehealth programs. Joining needs an Impact publisher account and brand-by-brand approval. The approval criteria (traffic, content type) were not visible.
- The FDA's Sept 2025 crackdown targeted ad claims like "same as Ozempic." Affiliates reusing that kind of copy for compounded products face the same claim risk.

### Gaps
- No official, dated terms for any telehealth program. Impact terms are only visible after login, and aggregator figures may be stale (LinkClicky pages are labeled 2024).
- No affiliate terms found for Eden (tryeden), Mochi Health or dedicated peptide clinics.
- No FTC actions specific to these telehealth affiliate programs found in this pass.

## Q3. Legal cosmetic / supplement peptide brands: what affiliate programs exist?

### Takeaway
Collagen and skincare peptide brands sit on mainstream networks (Awin, FlexOffers, in-house Shopify programs). Commissions run lower (about 4–25%) and cookies shorter, but regulatory risk is minimal. Ancient Nutrition's own page shows the best tiered rate (15–25%). Amazon Associates pays 3% for Beauty and Health & Personal Care and 10% for Luxury Beauty.

### Cited Findings
- **Vital Proteins (US):** 8% with a 7-day cookie. CA: 4% with a 14-day cookie. UK: 6–13% with a 30-day cookie (aggregators) — [FlexOffers US](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/vital-proteins-affiliate-program/); [FlexOffers CA](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/vital-proteins-ca-affiliate-program/); [TapRefer UK](https://taprefer.com/vital-proteins-uk-affiliate-program/vital-proteins-uk/); [Lasso UK](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/vital-proteins-uk/)
- **Ancient Nutrition (official page):** Seed tier ($0–$500/mo sales) 15%, Cultivator ($500–$2,499) 20%, Harvest ($2.5K+) 25%. Includes a $150+ welcome kit, a unique discount code, and quarterly product stipends for the upper tiers — [ancientnutrition.com/pages/affiliate](https://ancientnutrition.com/pages/affiliate). Network listings show lower rates (2.8–5%) — [Awin merchant 54585](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/54585); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/ancient-nutrition-affiliate-program/). This conflict may reflect network versus direct program terms.
- **Medik8:** 10% per sale, 30-day cookie, AOV £65. The Awin listing is UK-centric — [LinkClicky Medik8](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/medik8/); [Affiliate-Toolkit](https://www.affiliate-toolkit.com/program/medik8/)
- **The Ordinary / DECIEM (incl. NIOD):** up to 10% (some sources say 15% for top performers), 30-day cookie. DECIEM may change commission per campaign. Listed on FlexOffers and Awin — [FlexOffers The Ordinary](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/the-ordinary-affiliate-program/); [Awin terms 29849](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/29849); [Afftank skincare roundup 2026](https://afftank.com/blog/skincare-affiliate-programs). The Ordinary also runs a separate influencer/"Good Stuff" program — [theordinary.com/good-stuff](https://theordinary.com/en-us/good-stuff)
- **Amazon Associates (2026):** Luxury Beauty 10%, Beauty 3%, Health & Personal Care 3%. The 24-hour cookie extends to 90 days once an item is in the cart, and commission is paid on the whole cart — [Lasso Amazon rate guide](https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-commission-rate/); [AffiliateX](https://affiliatexblocks.com/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates/). The official Amazon fee schedule was not accessed.

### Inferences
- A compliant setup ("peptide skincare / collagen" content using Ancient Nutrition direct, Medik8/The Ordinary on Awin, and Amazon) pays less per sale, but those networks accept new publishers and there is no shutdown or enforcement risk like the RUO vendors carry.

### Gaps
- No US-specific Awin/FlexOffers terms opened for Medik8 or DECIEM. US availability of Medik8's program is unconfirmed.
- No reputation or enforcement check run for these cosmetic brands; I did not find FDA letters in this pass.

## Q4. Independent testing (Finnrick, Janoshik) and sentiment on vendor quality

### Takeaway
Finnrick publishes vendor-level pass rates. Default standard: HPLC purity of at least 99.5% and fill within ±35% of the label claim. In the snippets I saw, several affiliate-heavy vendors scored poorly, notably **Swiss Chems at 48%, ranked #279 of 283**. Claims of very high ratings for Core Peptides came from a vendor-affiliated blog in a format Finnrick does not seem to use, so they are suspect.

### Cited Findings
- Finnrick says partner labs analyze samples independently, that Finnrick sells no peptides, and that it does not accept payment to change ratings. Its default "Purity Focus" standard requires HPLC purity ≥99.5% and fill within ±35% of label — [Finnrick: Precision Peptide Co](https://preview.finnrick.com/vendors/precision-peptide-co)
- Swiss Chems: Finnrick rating 48%, #279 of 283 ranked vendors, 4 of 10 classifiable tests passed (snippet, date not shown) — [Finnrick: Swiss Chems](https://www.finnrick.com/vendors/swiss-chems); [Finnrick: Swiss Chems BPC-157](https://www.finnrick.com/products/bpc-157/swiss-chems)
- Limitless Life Nootropics: Finnrick rating 56%, 7 of 22 classifiable tests passed per snippet. A 56% rating with 7/22 passes looks inconsistent, possibly because ratings are volume-adjusted — [Finnrick: Limitless Life](https://www.finnrick.com/vendors/limitless-life-nootropics)
- As of Aug 9, 2026: Precision Peptide Co passed 15 of 21 tests and PeptiLab Research passed 10 of 15 — [Finnrick: Precision Peptide Co](https://preview.finnrick.com/vendors/precision-peptide-co); [Finnrick: PeptiLab](https://preview.finnrick.com/vendors/peptilab-research)
- Core Peptides "9.4/10, top 1%" comes from peptidestack.io, a site that appears to have commercial interests. Finnrick uses percentage ratings, so this claim is **unverified and possibly inaccurate** — [PeptideStack](https://peptidestack.io/blog/core-peptides-review-2026)

### Inferences
- Several popular affiliate-paying vendors have mediocre independent test records. An affiliate's reputation depends on vendor quality, so Finnrick should be checked before choosing a vendor.

### Gaps
- No Janoshik results gathered. Janoshik publishes per-sample certificates rather than vendor scores, and I found no aggregated source.
- No Trustpilot scores or structured Reddit sentiment collected, because the pages were inaccessible.
- Finnrick figures for Peptide Sciences, Amino Asylum, Biotech Peptides and Core Peptides were not retrieved.
